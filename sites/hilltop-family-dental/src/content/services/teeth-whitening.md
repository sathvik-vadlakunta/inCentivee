Coffee, tea, and time gradually dull your smile. Professional whitening at Hilltop lifts those stains safely and evenly — for results you simply can't get from a drugstore kit.

## Professional vs. over-the-counter

The American Dental Association notes that professional, dentist-supervised whitening uses higher-strength, regulated products and protects your gums and soft tissue — producing faster, more even results than one-size-fits-all store kits, with less risk of sensitivity.

## Your whitening options

- **In-office whitening** — dramatic results in a single visit, perfect before a wedding, reunion, or big event
- **Custom take-home trays** — professional-strength gel in trays made just for you, to whiten and touch up on your schedule

## Safe, comfortable, and supervised

We protect your gums and supervise the entire process. Any temporary sensitivity typically fades quickly, and we'll show you how to keep your smile bright for the long term.
