"As a dentist, I'm passionate about not only restoring and enhancing smiles, but also helping patients understand how to prevent issues so they can keep their smile for life."

Dr. Adam Milmont graduated from the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry and brings that preventive, education-first mindset to every patient at Hilltop Family Dentistry. He believes the best dentistry helps you avoid problems in the first place — and when treatment is needed, he delivers it with warmth and a gentle touch.

Known for putting even nervous patients at ease, Dr. Milmont is committed to compassionate, comprehensive care for the whole family.
