## Dental Implant Success Rate FAQ

### What does a 96.4% success rate mean for dental implants?

A 96.4% ten-year survival rate means that out of 100 dental implants placed, approximately 96 will still be functioning normally after 10 years. This includes both the implant itself and the ability to support a crown, bridge, or denture. This represents one of the highest success rates for any tooth replacement method.

### How do implant success rates compare to other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants have significantly higher success rates than removable partial dentures or traditional bridges. Bridges typically need replacement every 10-15 years, while properly maintained implants can last a lifetime. Implants also don't require alteration of adjacent healthy teeth, unlike traditional bridges.

### What factors can affect dental implant success?

Implant success depends on several factors: adequate bone density, good oral hygiene, non-smoking status, absence of untreated gum disease, and proper surgical technique. Medical conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or certain medications may also affect healing. Our South Jordan team carefully evaluates these factors before treatment.

### How can I maximize my implant success?

Maximize implant success by maintaining excellent oral hygiene, attending regular checkups, avoiding tobacco use, and following all post-surgical instructions. Patients who commit to these practices typically experience the best long-term outcomes with their dental implants.

Implant success rates have improved over time due to advances in implant design, surface treatments, and surgical techniques. In 2026, we benefit from decades of research and refinement that have made dental implants even more predictable and successful.

## Risk Factors and Patient Selection

While dental implants have high success rates overall, certain factors can influence outcomes. Understanding these factors helps ensure optimal results for South Jordan patients considering implant treatment.

[The CDC reports that approximately 1 in 4 adults in the US have untreated tooth decay](https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/php/2024-oral-health-surveillance-report/selected-findings.html), and addressing all oral health issues before implant placement contributes to better outcomes.

### Factors That May Affect Success:

- **Smoking:** Significantly reduces implant success rates
- **Uncontrolled diabetes:** Can impair healing and osseointegration
- **Active periodontal disease:** Must be treated before implant placement
- **Insufficient bone density:** May require grafting procedures first
- **Poor oral hygiene:** Increases risk of peri-implantitis
