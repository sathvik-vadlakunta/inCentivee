SoJo Dental creates custom dentures that restore your smile, confidence, and ability to eat comfortably. We offer both full and partial options, including implant-supported dentures for superior stability.

## Denture Options

### Full Dentures
Replace all teeth in the upper or lower arch. Custom-fabricated for a natural appearance and comfortable fit.

### Partial Dentures
Replace several missing teeth while preserving remaining natural teeth. Metal or flexible framework clasps to adjacent teeth for support.

### Implant-Supported Dentures
Dentures anchored by dental implants for a secure, stable fit. No adhesives needed. Significantly improved chewing ability and comfort compared to traditional dentures.

## Our Denture Process

1. Comprehensive evaluation and impressions
2. Custom shade and tooth selection
3. Try-in appointment for fit verification
4. Final denture delivery and adjustment
5. Follow-up visits to fine-tune fit and comfort
