# DentalRank Report System

## What It Produces

A 30-page SEO & AEO intelligence report for a dental practice. Scores the practice on:

- **Overall Score** (0-100)
- **Technical SEO** (0-100) — schema markup, sitemaps, page speed, mobile
- **Content Quality** (0-100) — depth, keyword coverage, content gaps
- **AEO Readiness** (0-100) — structured data for AI search engines
- **Local Visibility** (0-100) — Google Maps, citations, reviews, GBP
- **Tech Stack Health** (0-100) — CMS, hosting, CDN, SSL, analytics

## Report Sections

1. Executive Summary — headline score, key findings, competitive snapshot
2. Business Overview — practice positioning, patient demographics, business model
3. Local Market — competitor count, review economy, market saturation
4. AI Search Visibility — how practice appears in ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity
5. Current State Audit — technical SEO issues, content gaps
6. Competitive Landscape — side-by-side keyword/traffic/backlink comparison
7. Competitor Analysis — deep dive on top 3-5 competitors
8. Keyword Universe — full keyword inventory
9. Revenue-Driving Keywords — highest-value terms by intent and volume
10. Content Gap Analysis — keywords competitors rank for but practice doesn't
11. AEO/GEO Assessment — schema checklist, AI readiness score
12. Growth Opportunities — non-obvious plays (neighborhood pages, partnerships, video)
13. Technology & Performance — CMS, hosting, CDN, SSL, analytics stack
14. Strategy & Implementation Plan — 3-pillar roadmap with phased timeline
15. Projected Outcomes — 3/6/12 month growth milestones
16. Priority Recommendations — top 7 actions ranked by impact

## Sample Data (myaustindds.com — 38th Street Dental, Austin TX)

- Overall: 64/100
- 1,302 keywords, 1,296 monthly traffic, 1,243 backlinks
- AEO Readiness: 20/100 (no LocalBusiness schema, no FAQ schema)
- Missing: Full Mouth Reconstruction page, TMJ content, insurance pages
- Top competitor (River Rock Dental): 3,835 keywords, 4,279 traffic
- Content gap: 15+ keywords with 9,240 monthly searches where practice is invisible
- Projected 12-month outcome with full execution: 5,208 keywords, 5,184 traffic

## Data Sources Needed

- SEMrush or Ahrefs for keyword/traffic/backlink data
- Google PageSpeed Insights API for performance scores
- Schema.org validator for structured data audit
- Google Business Profile API for review counts
- Manual or automated CMS detection (BuiltWith, Wappalyzer)
