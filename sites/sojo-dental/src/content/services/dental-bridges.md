A dental bridge literally "bridges" the gap created by one or more missing teeth. At SoJo Dental, we create custom bridges that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

## Types of Bridges

### Traditional Bridge
The most common type. A pontic (false tooth) is held in place by dental crowns cemented to the teeth on either side of the gap.

### Implant-Supported Bridge
For patients missing three or more adjacent teeth. The bridge is anchored by dental implants instead of natural teeth, preserving healthy tooth structure.

## Benefits of Dental Bridges

- Restore your ability to chew and speak properly
- Maintain the shape of your face
- Prevent remaining teeth from shifting
- Distribute bite forces evenly
- Durable, long-lasting restoration

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I eat normally after getting a dental bridge?**

Yes, once the bridge is fully placed, you can enjoy most foods. We recommend starting with soft foods as you adjust.

**How do I clean around a dental bridge?**

Our dentists will demonstrate how to use floss threaders and other tools to clean underneath and around your bridge.

**Are cantilever bridges suitable for back teeth?**

They are generally better for front teeth due to less biting pressure, but each case is evaluated individually.

**Will my bridge look natural?**

Absolutely. We use materials that match your natural teeth in color, shape and size for a seamless look.
