Ryan Farmer is a skilled negotiator who works out of the firm's Bremen office. He spent many years under the Gold Dome of the Georgia State Capitol, where he served as **general counsel to the Senate Majority Leader** and counsel to the House Republican Caucus.

A graduate of the University of North Georgia and Mercer University School of Law, Ryan also serves as a District 4 representative on the Haralson County Board of Commissioners and as President of the Tallapoosa Bar Association. He handles personal injury, workers' compensation, and criminal defense matters.
