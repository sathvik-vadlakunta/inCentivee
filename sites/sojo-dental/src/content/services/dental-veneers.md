Porcelain veneers are one of the most effective ways to achieve a natural-looking, beautiful smile. At SoJo Dental, our doctors use high-quality dental porcelain to create custom veneers that match your natural tooth color, shape, and size.

## What Are Dental Veneers?

Veneers are ultra-thin shells of medical-grade ceramic that are bonded to the front surface of your teeth. They are custom-designed to cover imperfections and create a uniform, bright smile.

## Common Reasons for Veneers

- Chipped or broken teeth
- Gaps between teeth
- Severe discoloration that doesn't respond to whitening
- Worn down or uneven teeth
- Slightly misaligned teeth

## The Veneer Process at SoJo Dental

1. **Consultation** — We discuss your smile goals and examine your teeth to determine if veneers are right for you
2. **Preparation** — A small amount of enamel is removed to make room for the veneer
3. **Impressions** — Digital impressions are taken and sent to our lab for custom fabrication
4. **Bonding** — The veneers are permanently bonded to your teeth using dental cement

Our South Jordan office uses advanced digital imaging to help you preview your new smile before treatment begins.
