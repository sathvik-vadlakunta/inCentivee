Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime, but they require regular professional maintenance to stay healthy. At SoJo Dental, we provide specialized implant care to protect your investment and keep your smile functioning at its best.

## What Implant Maintenance Includes

- Professional cleaning around implant sites using specialized instruments
- Assessment of implant stability and surrounding bone health
- Digital X-rays to monitor bone integration and detect any issues early
- Evaluation of the implant crown, bridge, or denture attachment
- Personalized at-home care instructions for your implants

## Why Maintenance Matters

While implants cannot develop cavities, the surrounding gum tissue can become inflamed — a condition called peri-implantitis. Regular professional cleanings and exams help prevent this and catch any concerns before they become serious.

We recommend implant maintenance visits every six months, coordinated with your regular dental cleanings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How soon after an implant is placed should I begin maintenance?**

Maintenance begins once healing is complete, typically within a few months of placement.

**Can implant maintenance prevent future tooth loss?**

Yes, it helps protect gum and bone health, preventing issues that can affect other teeth.

**Are dental implant check-ups different from regular dental visits?**

Yes, they include specific assessments for implant stability and gum health around the implant.

**What tools are used to clean around implants?**

We use special non-metal instruments and ultrasonic devices safe for implant surfaces.

Yes. Our office provides dental implant maintenance in South Jordan, Utah, and for the surrounding areas of Herriman, Riverton and West Jordan, Utah.
