What shipped

Real brand — pulled from the live site: tan #a38f79 + slate #5c686e palette, their real white/reverse logo (dark slate nav), favicon, and real photography (logo, Casper mountains, office, doctor headshots, 17 practice photos).

Real doctors — actual headshots + real bios & education (Dr. Gallup — Indiana University; Dr. Milmont — University of Nebraska).
Real testimonials — 8 genuine Google reviews → homepage section, a /reviews page, and review cards beside service CTAs.
