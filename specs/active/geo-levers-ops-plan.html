GEO Levers — Automate vs Subcontract

How to operationalize the two highest-impact AI-visibility levers we're not doing yet — earned mentions / digital PR and local citations / NAP consistency — by automating what scales and subcontracting what doesn't, inside the ~$3k/mo PracticeRank margin (85%+ automated).

Owner: Kody · Status: Plan (draft) · 2026-06-17 · costs are market estimates to confirm.

Why these two (verified research)

  • Off-site branded mentions = the strongest lever — ~0.67 correlation with AI Overview appearance, ~3× backlinks (Ahrefs, 75k brands). We do none of this today.
  • For local queries, the business website (~58%) + consistent directory/NAP presence are the top AI citation sources (BrightLocal). We have the BrightLocal R4 scaffold but it isn't wired.

This is the most automatable lever — a citation aggregator (Moz Local or BrightLocal) does ~90% on autopilot; a VA handles the long-tail/vertical directories once.

The best plan — hybrid (automate-first, thin VA layer)

Automate the scalable 85%, subcontract the relationship 15%, to one shared part-time VA — not a per-client agency.

Stack

  • Citations/NAP: Moz Local or BrightLocal (wire our R4) — set-and-monitor. + VA one-time cleanup per new customer.
  • Digital PR: in-house Claude drafting (PR + HARO responses + roundup pitches) → one shared VA sends/follows up across all customers (~10–15 hrs/wk total) → EIN Presswire for monthly distribution.
  • Loop: the weekly report's "Sources now citing you" already shows which earned mentions AI picked up → prioritize what works.

Against ~$3,000/mo pricing that's ~2.5–5% of revenue — well within the 85%+-automated model. Scales: tools + Claude are fixed-ish; the VA layer grows slowly with the book (1 VA per ~15–25 customers).

How it makes the offer stickier

  • Add to the monthly deliverable: "N new earned mentions + NAP kept 100% consistent across M directories" — concrete, recurring proof alongside the weekly report's climbing AI Share-of-Voice.
  • These levers compound (mentions + citations accrue), so churning means losing accumulated visibility — the retention hook.
  • Reporting: surface "sources now citing you" growth + citation-consistency score in the one weekly report.

Rollout

  1. Pick the citation tool (Moz Local vs BrightLocal — we already scaffolded BrightLocal R4); wire it + auto-pull into the report's "Local listings health".
  2. Stand up the digital-PR loop: Claude prompt templates for PR/HARO/roundup pitches + a tracking sheet; hire 1 shared VA; pick a wire.
  3. Close the loop: tie earned mentions to the "sources now citing you" data so we double down on what AI actually cites.
  4. Add both as line items to the service spec + client offer.

Decisions for you

  • Citation tool: BrightLocal (reuse R4 scaffold) vs Moz Local (cheaper/simpler)?
  • PR cadence: monthly release per client vs quarterly + continuous HARO (cheaper)?
  • VA: hire now, or start with HARO + citations only and add the VA at ~10 customers?
