StemWave

StemWave is a regenerative medicine technique that utilizes harmless, yet powerful acoustic waves to activate your body’s natural healing mechanisms. StemWave is the latest, more powerful version of Shockwave therapy. Shockwave Therapy has been used in doctors offices for over 20 years. It is designed to help patients with musculoskeletal conditions by increasing blood flow and reducing inflammation. As a safe non-invasive option, it doesn’t require any downtime and has minimal or no side-effects.

Dry Needling

Dry-needling is a technique used by physical therapists using a thin filiform needle. The needle penetrates tissues and stimulates underlying myofascial trigger points. Dry needling is not acupuncture. Acupuncture is based in Traditional Chinese Medicine and uses meridian energy techniques. Dry-needling allows a physical therapist to reach trigger points within the muscle that they may not be able to reach with their hands. Dry needling is used to activate immobile muscles and relieve pain.
