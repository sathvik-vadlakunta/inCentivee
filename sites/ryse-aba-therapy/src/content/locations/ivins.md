## ABA Services We Bring to Ivins

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is an evidence-based approach for autism, and the design and oversight of every program is led by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) — a credential governed by the [Behavior Analyst Certification Board](https://www.bacb.com/). In Ivins, our team provides:

- One-on-one, in-home ABA sessions tailored to your child
- Personalized, data-driven treatment plans built and supervised by a BCBA
- Communication, daily-living, social, and play skill-building
- Parent and caregiver coaching so progress continues between visits
- Community-based support in the places your family already goes

We serve a wide age range — **ages 2 through 65** — so toddlers, school-age kids, teens, and adults can all access care. Many providers stop at young children; we don't.

## Why In-Home ABA Works for Ivins

Children tend to learn best where they feel safe and where the skills actually matter — at home, at the kitchen table, in the backyard, on a trip to the store. In-home ABA lets your child practice in their natural environment, which can help new skills carry over into everyday routines. It also means **no commute** for your family and a setting your child already trusts. For Ivins parents balancing work, siblings, and a busy household, having care come to you removes one of the biggest barriers to getting started.

According to the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/autism/treatment/index.html), there are several evidence-based approaches for supporting autistic individuals, and the right plan depends on each person's individual needs — which is exactly why our BCBA builds your child's program around them, not a template.
