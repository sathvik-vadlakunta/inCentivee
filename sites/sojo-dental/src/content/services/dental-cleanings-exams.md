Regular dental cleanings and exams are the foundation of good oral health. At SoJo Dental, our hygiene team provides thorough, gentle cleanings while our doctors perform comprehensive exams to catch problems early.

## What's Included

- **Professional cleaning** — removal of plaque, tartar, and stains
- **Comprehensive exam** — evaluation of teeth, gums, bite, and jaw
- **Digital X-rays** — low-radiation imaging to detect hidden issues
- **Oral cancer screening** — visual and tactile exam of soft tissues
- **Personalized care plan** — recommendations based on your unique needs

## Why Preventive Care Matters

Catching dental issues early saves time, money, and discomfort. Regular visits allow us to identify cavities, gum disease, and other concerns before they become serious problems.

We welcome patients of all ages, from toddlers to seniors, at our South Jordan office.
