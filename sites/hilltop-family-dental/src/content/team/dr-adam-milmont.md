"As a dentist, I'm passionate about not only restoring and enhancing smiles, but also helping patients understand how to prevent issues so they can keep their smile for life."

Born and raised in Wyoming, Dr. Adam Milmont is proud to care for his home-state community. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry and brings a preventive, education-first mindset to every patient at Hilltop Family Dentistry. He believes the best dentistry helps you avoid problems in the first place — and when treatment is needed, he delivers it with warmth and a gentle touch.

Known for putting even nervous patients at ease, Dr. Milmont is committed to compassionate, comprehensive care for the whole family.

## Outside the office

Dr. Milmont and his wife Susan have five children. Together they enjoy camping, playing games, and spending time outdoors — and Dr. Milmont is an avid woodworker who loves building things by hand.
