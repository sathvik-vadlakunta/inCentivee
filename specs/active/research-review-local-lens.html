Re-reading the verified findings through the lens of our actual clients: single-location (or few-location) dental practices, law firms, and local retail (e.g., a gold/silver buyer) serving a city + ~25-min radius — not national brands. Which findings transfer, which were national-skewed, and how to re-prioritize.

The lens (why this matters)

Most AI-search/GEO studies pool all brands (often national B2B/SaaS with real brand-search volume and PR budgets). A local dentist or coin shop behaves differently: tiny brand-search volume, no national press, a handful of pages, and customers asking "near me / in {city}". So we weight the local-specific evidence highest and treat national-brand findings as directional.

⭐ The lever the national research UNDER-weights: REVIEWS

For local businesses, Google review volume, rating, and recency are a top AI-recommendation signal — and a local-specific one the all-brand studies barely capture. AI assistants lean on review trust for "best/near me" local queries.

  • This validates keeping Reputation in the Score (rating + review growth) — but for local it's arguably co-equal with off-site mentions, not a 10% afterthought.
  • Actionable: a review-generation motion (post-visit/post-transaction review requests) is a high-ROI local lever we should run alongside earned mentions — and it's cheap/automatable.
  • Recency matters: a steady drip of fresh reviews beats a stale pile — pairs with the review-growth-trend signal we already score.

Refuted findings — even less relevant for local

  • Reddit / Wikipedia dominance — national/B2B phenomenon. A local dentist or coin shop won't (and shouldn't try to) rank via Reddit threads or get a Wikipedia page. Skip entirely for local.
  • Princeton "+22%/+37%" format lifts, comparative-listicle %s — general/national and unverified. The tactics (quotes, stats, FAQ) are still fine qualitatively; the numbers aren't.
  • "13-week freshness rule" — unverified and, for local, contradicted by the lighter cadence local sites actually need.
  • Specific niche directories (Three Best Rated, Expertise) — unverified ranking; for local, prioritize Google/Apple/Yelp/Bing + the vertical dirs (Healthgrades/Zocdoc, Avvo/Justia, BBB/coin-dealer dirs) instead.

Implications for PracticeRank

  • Lead the offer with the BrightLocal-backed local facts (website = #1 local citation source; 45% use AI for local recs) — these are local-specific and defensible.
  • Add a review-generation motion as a first-class lever for local clients (high ROI, cheap, automatable) — don't treat reviews as a minor score input.
  • Scope earned mentions to LOCAL outlets (local press / regional / niche), and tell vendors so (question #8 in the vendor spec). National placements are wasted spend for a local business.
  • Lighten the content-refresh cadence to quarterly for most local clients (money pages excepted) — saves cost and matches the evidence.
  • Drop Reddit/Wikipedia ambitions for local — not worth the effort for single-location businesses.

Net: the local-specific evidence (BrightLocal) reinforces our Foundation + Lever B + reviews; the national-skewed findings (Ahrefs mentions, Seer cadence) still apply but get re-scoped to local outlets and a lighter cadence. Reviews deserve a promotion.
