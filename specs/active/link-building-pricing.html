The DR reality (so we don't oversell it)

  • DR is logarithmic. The first handful of links give the biggest jump. A DR0.1 local site typically climbs into the 8–15 range off ~100 citations + 3–4 DR20–30 links. 0→10 is cheap and fast; 30→40 is where it gets expensive — and our clients don't need that.
  • DR is Ahrefs' metric, not Google's. Links help rankings and AI trust, but for local clients the GBP + citations + reviews do most of the local-ranking work (our verified research). Frame links as "get them off zero authority," not "DR is the goal."
  • Relevance > raw DR for local. A DR25 local-news / chamber / industry link beats a random DR55 general blog for a Springfield gold buyer. And a natural profile is a mix of DRs — all-high-DR looks engineered.

How it plugs into the app

  • Citations fulfill the Local SEO tab's citation steps (Lever B / NAP). FATJOE's citation build ($1–1.50/site) is cheaper than BrightLocal's Citation Builder — likely split: BrightLocal API for tracking/NAP audit, FATJOE for building.
  • Links + brand mentions fulfill Lever A (off-site authority) and show up in the weekly report's "sources now citing you" once live.
  • Future: automate ordering via FATJOE's reseller dashboard/API so the VA (or the engine) places orders straight from the Local SEO tab.

Rollout

  1. Open a FATJOE reseller account; confirm white-label reports + bulk discount.
  2. Pilot on one client per vertical (Sojo dental, a legal, Paradigm retail): 100 citations + 3 DR20–30 relevant links; baseline their DR in Ahrefs.
  3. Re-check DR + local rankings at 30/60 days; confirm the lift before scaling.
  4. Turn on the base bundle for all active clients; price the four add-ons into the sales deck.
  5. Decide BrightLocal-vs-FATJOE for citation building once both accounts exist.
Bottom line: white-label all three, bundle a light citation + 1–2 relevant-link/month baseline into the $3k (≈8% COGS), and upsell the rest. Ignore the "10/month" pitch — for low-DR local sites, a few relevant links beat a firehose, and relevance beats raw DR.
