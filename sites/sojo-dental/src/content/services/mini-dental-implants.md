Mini dental implants (MDIs) offer a less invasive alternative to traditional implants. Their smaller diameter makes them ideal for patients with limited bone density or those seeking a faster, more affordable solution.

## Advantages of Mini Implants

- **Minimally invasive** — smaller incision, less bone required
- **Faster procedure** — often placed in a single visit
- **Quicker healing** — less recovery time than standard implants
- **Lower cost** — more affordable option for stabilizing dentures
- **Immediate function** — dentures can often be attached the same day

## Common Uses

- Stabilizing loose lower dentures
- Replacing small teeth in narrow spaces
- Patients with insufficient bone for standard implants
- Temporary anchorage during orthodontic treatment

Dr. Brian Call evaluates each patient to determine whether mini implants or traditional implants are the best option for their situation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are mini dental implants suitable for everyone?**

They are ideal for patients with limited bone structure or those seeking a less invasive procedure. A consultation will confirm your eligibility.

**Can mini implants be used for dentures?**

Yes, mini implants are frequently used to secure dentures, improving fit and comfort.

**Do mini implants require special care?**

No, they can be cared for just like natural teeth with regular brushing and flossing.

**How soon can I eat after the procedure?**

Most patients resume eating soft foods within a day and return to normal diets after a few days.
