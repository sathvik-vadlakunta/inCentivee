"I think patients deserve comfortable care in the most efficient way." That philosophy guides everything Dr. David Gallup does at Hilltop Family Dentistry.

Originally from Swan Valley, Idaho, Dr. Gallup earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the Indiana University School of Dentistry and settled in Casper, where he's proud to own and lead Hilltop Family Dentistry. He combines a gentle, patient-first approach with modern techniques to make every visit comfortable and efficient — whether it's a routine checkup, a cosmetic makeover, or restoring a smile with dental implants.

Patients appreciate that Dr. Gallup takes the time to listen, explain their options clearly, and deliver honest, high-quality care.

## Outside the office

Dr. Gallup and his wife have five children, and the family loves taking full advantage of the outdoor adventures Wyoming has to offer.
