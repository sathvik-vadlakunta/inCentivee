Physical Therapy Experts in Nevada

Are you tired of long wait times to see a healthcare practitioner, only to have limited time with them and spend most of the visit with support staff?

Do you feel frustrated by being blocked by front office staff or having to leave multiple messages when you have a simple question for your physical therapist?

Look no further—we can help.

Healing Hands Physical Therapy and Bodywork offers a different approach to healthcare—one that honors your time, your needs, and your unique healing journey. We provide Concierge Physical Therapy so our physical therapy experts can spend quality, one-on-one time with each client to deliver the highest level of care.

How Concierge Healthcare is different:

  • Our doctors spend an average of one hour with each client at every appointment.

  • Tranquil, welcoming, warm atmosphere—we care.

  • Your care plan is determined by you and the doctor, not dictated by insurance.

  • Helping you find pain relief is our main goal.

  • Return to doing what you love as quickly as possible.

  • The office phone is answered by experienced front office professionals.

  • You will have a direct line of communication with your doctor via phone, text, or email.

  • We can come to you! Mobile visits are available for the Reno-Sparks area.

Our goal at Healing Hands Physical Therapy and Bodywork is to facilitate healing and restore efficient movement and comfort as quickly as possible. If you’re looking for expert physical therapy in Reno, our experienced therapists provide hands-on techniques, one-on-one therapy, and personalized treatment plans to help you heal and move with ease.

We use a variety of hands-on techniques, including:

  • Joint mobilizations

  • StemWave treatments

  • Muscle energy techniques

  • Soft tissue mobilizations

  • Myofascial release

  • CranioSacral therapy

  • Dry needling

  • Proprioceptive Neuromuscular Facilitation (PNF)

  • Rehabilitation exercises

Physical Therapy Experts in Nevada

Dr. Jamie Pribyl

PT, DPT, MTC

Physical Therapist, Owner

Dr. Jamie is a native Nevadan. She attended UNR for her Bachelor of Health Sciences in Health Ecology. Then she received her Doctorate of Physical Therapy from the University of St. Augustine in 2010. She continued to get her Manual Therapy Certification from the University of St. Augustine in 2012.

Since then, manual therapy and bodywork have been her passion. She is proficient in myofascial release, muscle energy technique, CranioSacral therapy, dry needling, strain/counter strain, and many other modalities. As a natural healer with a deep understanding of body mechanics, Dr. Jamie brings over 15 years of experience to Healing Hands Physical Therapy and Bodywork.

In her free time, she enjoys spending time with her family. She has two young boys and enjoys being active in the great outdoors of the Sierra Nevadas.

We provide concierge Physical Therapy services in Reno, Sparks, Carson City and Incline Village, NV.
