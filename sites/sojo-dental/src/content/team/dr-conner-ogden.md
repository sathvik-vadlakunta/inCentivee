Dr. Conner Ogden brings compassion and professionalism to every patient interaction at SoJo Dental. With advanced training and a commitment to continuing education, he stays at the forefront of modern dental care with expertise in general, pediatric and endodontic treatment.

Dr. Ogden has a natural ability to put patients — especially children — at ease. He takes time to explain procedures, answer questions and make sure each patient understands their treatment options. His gentle approach to pediatric dentistry helps young patients develop positive associations with dental care from an early age.

Fluent in Spanish, Dr. Ogden is passionate about creating a welcoming and inclusive environment. He is experienced in wisdom teeth removal and root canal therapy, rounding out SoJo Dental's ability to handle a wide range of dental needs in-house. Patients appreciate not only his skill but also his down-to-earth personality and genuine dedication to lifelong oral health.
