Dr. Brian Call brings a unique blend of experience, compassion and professionalism to every patient interaction at SoJo Dental. With advanced training in implant dentistry, cosmetic procedures and IV sedation, he stays at the forefront of modern dental care through a commitment to continuing education.

Whether addressing preventive needs or complex restorative concerns, he delivers the highest standard of care with a down-to-earth personality and genuine dedication to lifelong oral health.
