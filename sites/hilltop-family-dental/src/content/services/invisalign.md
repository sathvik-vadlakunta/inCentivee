Want a straighter smile without the look of traditional braces? Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom aligners to gently move your teeth into place — comfortably and discreetly.

## Why patients love Invisalign

- **Virtually invisible** — clear aligners most people won't even notice
- **Removable** — take them out to eat, brush, and floss as normal
- **Comfortable** — smooth aligners instead of metal brackets and wires
- **Predictable** — digital planning lets you preview your future smile before you begin

## Straighter teeth are healthier teeth

Beyond appearance, properly aligned teeth are easier to clean and less prone to wear and gum problems — so Invisalign is an investment in your long-term oral health, not just your smile.

## What to expect

We take a digital scan and map out your entire treatment, then create your custom aligners. You'll switch to a new set every couple of weeks, with check-ins to keep everything on track. Most cases finish in 6–18 months.
