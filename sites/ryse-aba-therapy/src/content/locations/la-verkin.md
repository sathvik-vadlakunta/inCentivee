## In-Home ABA Therapy for La Verkin Families

If you're a parent in La Verkin searching for ABA therapy and bracing for a months-long wait, here's the relief you came for: **Ryse ABA Therapy has no waitlist — families start right away.** We bring personalized, play-based, data-driven Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) into your home and community, so your child gets support where they already live and learn — no drive to a center in St. George or Hurricane required.

## ABA Services Available in La Verkin

Ryse serves children, teens, and adults — **ages 2 through 65** — a wider range than many providers who stop at young kids. In and around La Verkin, families can access:

- **Early intervention** for toddlers and young children
- **In-home ABA** built around your family's real routines
- **Community-based ABA** at the places you go — the park, the store, school transitions
- **Social skills** support
- **Parent and caregiver training**, so progress continues between sessions

Every plan is individualized and led by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA). ABA is an evidence-based, individualized approach — we never promise cures or guaranteed outcomes; we focus on meaningful, measurable goals built around your child.
