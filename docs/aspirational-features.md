# Aspirational Features (from Playbook)

These are ideas from the AI Dental Marketing Platform Playbook. They are NOT the core product — they represent expansion opportunities and are listed here for future reference.

## PMS Integration

Direct integration with dental Practice Management Software:
- **Dentrix**, **Open Dental** (best API), **Eaglesoft**
- Middleware: DentalBridge ($99 setup, waived at 10+) or CRMBridge (free tier)
- Syncs patient visit data to CRM for automated follow-up triggers

## Dental Tourism Content

- Spanish-language content targeting Mexico City/Monterrey residents seeking premium US dental care
- $2 billion market segment
- Natural fit for premium/fee-for-service practices

## Interactive Tools

- **Smile Cost Calculator** — patients estimate treatment costs, generates leads + backlinks
- **Smile Assessment Tool** — interactive quiz generating qualified consultation requests

## Video Content Strategy

- Procedure walkthroughs
- Patient testimonials
- "Day in the Life" content
- YouTube channel as differentiation (virtually no Austin dental practices doing this)

## Partnership Channels

- Wedding planners (3,000+ Austin weddings/year → smile makeover packages, $5k-15k LTV)
- Medical spas (shared aesthetic clientele)
- Corporate wellness programs at tech companies (Dell, Oracle, Indeed)

## Neighborhood/Hyperlocal Pages

- Programmatic "Cosmetic Dentist in [Neighborhood]" pages
- Target 20+ affluent enclaves per metro
- Estimated 5,000+ monthly visitors from hyperlocal searches

## Insurance Navigation Hub

- Pages for "Aetna dental coverage [city]", "Delta Dental dentist [city]"
- Captures 2,000+ monthly searches from patients confirming coverage
- High-intent, pre-decision traffic

## Niche Content Opportunities

- Holistic/mercury-free dentistry (searches growing 40% YoY)
- Musician's embouchure problems
- Tech worker TMJ (ergonomic/stress-related)
- Dental anxiety resource center
