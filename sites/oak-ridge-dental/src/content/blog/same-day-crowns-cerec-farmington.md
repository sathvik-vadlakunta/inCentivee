At **Oakridge Dental in Farmington**, **CEREC technology** lets us skip all of that and hand you a permanent, custom-fitted crown in a **single visit**.

Here's everything you need to know about same-day crowns — how they work, how they compare to the traditional approach, and whether one is right for you.

## First, what is a dental crown — and why might you need one?

A [dental crown](/services/dental-crowns-bridges) is a custom "cap" that covers a damaged tooth, restoring its shape, strength, and appearance. Think of it as a protective helmet for a tooth that can no longer stand on its own.

You might need a crown if you have:

- A **cracked, chipped, or broken** tooth
- A tooth weakened by a large [filling](/services/dental-fillings) or decay
- A tooth that's had [root canal therapy](/services/root-canal-therapy) (which leaves it more brittle)
- Significant wear from grinding
- A cosmetic concern you'd like to correct

Crowns are one of the most common — and most important — restorative treatments in dentistry, because they let you keep your natural tooth instead of losing it.

## Traditional crowns vs. same-day CEREC crowns

The end result is similar, but the experience is very different:

| | Traditional crown | CEREC same-day crown |
|---|---|---|
| **Appointments** | 2 visits | 1 visit |
| **Temporary crown** | Yes (1–2 weeks) | None needed |
| **Impressions** | Putty molds | Digital scan |

For most people, fewer appointments and no temporary crown is a meaningful upgrade — especially if you have a busy schedule or some dental anxiety.

## How CEREC same-day crowns work

CEREC stands for **C**hairside **E**conomical **R**estoration of **E**sthetic **C**eramics. The technology has been refined since the first chairside CAD/CAM ceramic restoration was placed in 1985, and [Dentsply Sirona](https://www.dentsplysirona.com/en-us/explore/cad-cam/product-topics/cerec.html) reports that more than 50 million CEREC restorations have been placed worldwide. In plain terms, it's a system that lets us design and create your crown right here in the office. Here's the process:

1. **Digital scan.** Instead of biting into messy impression putty, we capture a precise 3D digital scan of your tooth in minutes.
2. **Design.** Using that scan, we design your crown on-screen — shaping it to match your bite and the contours of your natural teeth.
3. **Milling.** An in-office milling unit carves your crown from a solid block of tooth-colored ceramic. This usually takes only a matter of minutes.
4. **Fit & bond.** We check the fit and your bite, make any fine adjustments, and bond the finished crown permanently in place.

You walk out the same day with a strong, natural-looking crown — no temporary, no waiting, no return trip.

## Are same-day crowns actually as good?

It's a fair question, and the answer is yes for the vast majority of teeth. CEREC crowns are milled from **a single solid block of high-grade ceramic**, so there are no weak points. They're:

- **Strong and durable** — well-suited to the chewing forces of back teeth
- **Natural-looking** — tooth-colored and color-matched to blend in
- **Metal-free** — a biocompatible, esthetic option
- **Precisely fitted** — digital design means an accurate, comfortable bite

In a few specialized situations — certain highly cosmetic front-tooth cases, for example — a traditional lab crown may still be the better choice. We'll always recommend whatever gives *your* tooth the best result.

## What to expect at your appointment

Plan for roughly two hours. Here's how the visit flows:

- We numb the tooth so you're completely comfortable.
- We gently prepare the tooth and take the digital scan.
- While the crown is designed and milled, you can relax — many patients are surprised it all happens in one sitting.
- We bond the crown, check your bite, and you're done.

A little sensitivity for a few days afterward is normal as the tooth settles. Over-the-counter pain relief is rarely needed but works well if you want it.

## Caring for your crown

A CEREC crown doesn't ask for anything special — just good everyday care:

- Brush twice a day and floss daily, including around the crown.
- Keep up with regular [cleanings and exams](/services) so we can monitor it.
- Avoid using your teeth as tools (opening packages, chewing ice, etc.).
- If you grind your teeth, ask us about a night guard to protect both your crown and your natural teeth.

With that care, a same-day crown commonly lasts 10–15 years or more.
