About Dr. Paul Zhivago's Qualifications

What does FACP after Dr. Zhivago's name mean?

FACP stands for Fellow of the American College of Prosthodontists, representing the highest level of achievement in prosthodontic specialty. This fellowship recognizes Dr. Zhivago's advanced training, clinical expertise, and contributions to the field of tooth replacement and restoration.

What is Dr. Zhivago's role at NYU College of Dentistry?

Dr. Zhivago serves as Clinical Associate Professor in the Department of Prosthodontics and Course Director of Digital Dentistry for the Postgraduate Prosthodontics program at NYU College of Dentistry. He teaches the next generation of prosthodontists about advanced digital techniques and modern dental technology.

Where did Dr. Zhivago receive his implant training?

Dr. Zhivago completed his implant training at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine, one of the leading dental schools in the country. This advanced training complements his prosthodontic specialty in providing comprehensive tooth replacement solutions.

Does Dr. Zhivago lecture on his expertise?

Yes, Dr. Zhivago lectures nationally and internationally on digital dentistry and advanced prosthodontic techniques. His expertise in digital workflow and 3D printing technology is recognized throughout the dental community.

What makes a prosthodontist different from a general dentist?

Prosthodontists like Dr. Zhivago receive 2-3 years of additional training beyond dental school, specializing exclusively in tooth replacement, restoration, and complex bite relationships. This advanced training makes prosthodontists uniquely qualified for challenging cases involving multiple missing teeth, full mouth reconstruction, and aesthetic smile design.
