## You're not alone in wanting straighter teeth

Crooked teeth and bite problems are extremely common. Research estimates that **malocclusion
(misaligned teeth or bite) affects roughly 44–67% of people**, and surveys suggest **well
over half of Americans could benefit from some orthodontic treatment**. The good news:
straightening teeth as an adult is more discreet and convenient than ever.

## How Invisalign works

Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners that gently shift your teeth into
place. You wear each set for about two weeks, then move to the next — gradually guiding your
smile to where it should be.

- **Virtually invisible** — most people won't even notice you're wearing them
- **Removable** — take them out to eat, brush, and floss as normal
- **Comfortable** — smooth aligners instead of metal brackets and wires
- **Predictable** — digital planning lets you preview your final smile before you start

## How long does it take, and does it work?

Most cases finish in **6–18 months**. In one comparative study, adults completed clear-aligner
treatment in about **14.5 months on average** — comparable to braces. Systematic reviews and
meta-analyses find clear aligners **effective for correcting many malocclusions**, with the
added benefits of comfort and convenience.

## Straighter teeth are healthier teeth

Beyond appearance, properly aligned teeth are **easier to clean** and **less prone to wear,
chips, and gum problems**. So Invisalign is an investment in your long-term oral health, not
just your smile.

## Who is a good candidate?

Invisalign works well for most common concerns — crowding, gaps, and many bite issues. Very
complex cases may still be better suited to other treatment, which is exactly what your
consultation is for. We'll take a digital scan, map out your treatment, and show you what's
realistic before you commit.
