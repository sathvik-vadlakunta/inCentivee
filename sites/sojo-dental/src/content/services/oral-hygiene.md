Good oral hygiene is the foundation of a healthy smile. At SoJo Dental, we provide personalized guidance to help every patient develop and maintain effective at-home care routines.

## Our Approach

During each visit, our hygienists assess your brushing and flossing habits, identify areas that need extra attention, and provide hands-on instruction tailored to your unique needs. We believe that education is just as important as treatment.

## Key Recommendations

- Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste for at least two minutes
- Floss daily to remove plaque and food particles between teeth
- Use an antimicrobial mouthwash to reduce bacteria
- Replace your toothbrush every 3-4 months
- Limit sugary and acidic foods and beverages
- Schedule regular dental cleanings every six months

Our team is always available to answer questions about products, techniques, and habits that support your oral health between appointments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How effective are dental sealants in preventing cavities?

[Dental sealants reduce the risk of cavities in molars by nearly 80% in the first two years](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/65/wr/mm6541e1.htm), according to CDC Vital Signs data. At Sojo Dental in South Jordan, we recommend sealants for children and teens as a highly effective preventive measure to protect permanent molars from decay.

### How many Americans skip regular dental care?

[According to the American Dental Association, 100 million Americans fail to see a dentist each year](https://www.ada.org/resources/research/health-policy-institute). This statistic highlights why we emphasize the importance of regular preventive care at our South Jordan practice. Routine cleanings and exams help prevent small problems from becoming painful, expensive emergencies.

### When should children have their first dental visit?

[Children should have their first dental visit by age 1 or within 6 months of their first tooth erupting](https://www.aapd.org/resources/parent/faq/), according to the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. Early dental visits help establish good oral hygiene habits and allow us to monitor proper tooth development from the beginning.

### What percentage of adults have untreated tooth decay?

[The CDC reports that approximately 1 in 4 adults in the US have untreated tooth decay](https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/php/2024-oral-health-surveillance-report/selected-findings.html). This demonstrates the critical importance of regular dental checkups and preventive care. Our team at Sojo Dental helps South Jordan patients maintain optimal oral health through comprehensive preventive programs.
