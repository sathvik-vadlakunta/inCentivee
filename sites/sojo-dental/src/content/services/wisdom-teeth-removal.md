Wisdom teeth often lack the space to erupt properly, leading to impaction, crowding, and infection. Our team at SoJo Dental performs safe, efficient wisdom teeth extractions with sedation options for your comfort.

## When Removal Is Recommended

- Impacted wisdom teeth (trapped below the gumline)
- Partial eruption causing recurring gum infections
- Crowding or shifting of adjacent teeth
- Cysts or damage to surrounding bone

## What to Expect

We begin with a digital X-ray evaluation to plan the best approach. IV sedation is available so you remain comfortable throughout. Most patients return to normal activities within a few days, following our detailed post-operative care instructions.
