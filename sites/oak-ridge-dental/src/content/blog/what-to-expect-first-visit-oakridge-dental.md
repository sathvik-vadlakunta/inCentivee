Trying a new dental office can feel like a small leap of faith — you don't quite know what to expect, who you'll meet, or whether you'll feel comfortable. At **Oakridge Dental in Farmington**, we've built our entire first-visit experience around one goal: making you feel welcome, informed, and genuinely cared for. Here's exactly how it works, start to finish.

## Before you arrive

A little preparation makes your first visit smooth and stress-free:

- **Paperwork.** We'll provide new-patient forms ahead of time, or you can arrive about 10–15 minutes early to complete them in our office. (See our [New Patients](/new-patients) page for details.)
- **What to bring:**
  - A photo ID
  - Your dental insurance card, if you have one
  - A list of any medications you take
  - Recent X-rays or records from a previous dentist, if available

Not sure about your insurance or have questions before you come in? Just call us at **[(385) 600-6715](tel:+13856006715)** — our front-office team is happy to help.

## A warm welcome

From the moment you walk in, you'll be greeted by name. Our front-desk team — friendly faces you'll get to know over the years — will get you settled, answer any questions, and make sure you're comfortable before anything begins. (And yes, there may be a warm cookie waiting on your way out.)

## Your comprehensive exam

Your first visit is more thorough than a typical cleaning appointment, because we're getting to know your mouth for the first time. Your dentist will:

- **Examine your teeth** for decay, cracks, worn fillings, and other issues.
- **Check your gums** for signs of gum disease, which is one of the most common — and most preventable — dental problems.
- **Screen for oral cancer.** A quick, painless check that can catch problems early, when they're most treatable.
- **Review your bite and jaw** for signs of grinding, clenching, or TMJ concerns.
- **Take digital X-rays as needed.** Modern digital imaging uses very low radiation and gives us a complete picture of what's happening below the surface.

We use advanced technology — from 3D imaging to intraoral cameras — so you can actually *see* what we see. There's nothing mysterious about it; we'll walk you through everything on screen.

## Your cleaning

In most cases, we'll clean your teeth the same day. A professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing can't reach, polishes away surface stains, and leaves your smile feeling fresh.

If your exam reveals signs of gum disease, we may recommend a deeper cleaning at a follow-up visit so we can treat the issue properly rather than rushing it. Either way, we'll explain why and what to expect.

## A personalized plan — no pressure

After your exam and cleaning, we'll sit down and talk. You'll learn:

- What we found, in plain language
- Which issues (if any) need attention now versus what we can simply monitor
- Your options for any recommended treatment — with the pros, cons, and costs of each
- How insurance and financing apply

We believe good dentistry is a partnership. You'll never feel pushed into treatment — our job is to give you the information and let you decide what's right for you and your budget.

## Bringing the kids? Here's what to know

Oakridge Dental is a true family practice, and we love seeing children. The [American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry](https://www.aapd.org/resources/parent/) recommends a child's first dental visit by their first birthday — within six months of their first tooth coming in. A child's first visit is gentle and unhurried — focused on making the dentist a friendly, positive place. We'll count and check their teeth, offer a gentle cleaning if they're ready, and give you guidance on home care at every age. The earlier kids learn that the dentist is nothing to fear, the better their oral health tends to be for life.

## Nervous about the dentist? You're not alone

Dental anxiety is extremely common, and it's nothing to be embarrassed about. We offer a calm, patient approach and several comfort options, including [nitrous oxide](/services/nitrous-oxide), [oral sedation](/services/oral-sedation), and [IV sedation](/services/iv-sedation). Just let us know you're nervous when you book — we'll take the extra time to make sure you're at ease.
