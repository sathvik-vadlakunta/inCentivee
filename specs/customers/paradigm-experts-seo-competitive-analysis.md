## Why Alexandria Wins

### 1. Homepage Authority Monster
Alexandria's homepage alone ranks for **485 keywords** and drives **1,267 monthly visits**. Their homepage URL (`alexandriagoldandsilver.com/`) captures the vast majority of their organic traffic.

Paradigm's homepage ranks for only **87 keywords** with **112 traffic** (and 17 of that is from the `http://` version, indicating a canonical issue).

### 2. "Near Me" Domination
Alexandria owns **49 "near me" keywords** in the top 10, including massive volume terms:
- "gold and silver buyers near me" — pos 2 (4,400 vol)
- "buying silver near me" — pos 9 (2,400 vol)
- "silver dealers near me" — pos 7 (1,900 vol)
- "gold and silver shops near me" — pos 7 (1,900 vol)
- "gold silver buyers near me" — pos 2 (1,600 vol)

Paradigm ranks for **zero** of these keywords. Not page 2. Not page 5. They simply don't appear at all.

### 3. Transactional Intent Capture
Alexandria's top-10 keywords are 70%+ transactional ("sell silver near me", "cash for gold near me", "where to sell gold"). These are the highest-converting keywords — people ready to walk in and sell.

Paradigm's rankings are dominated by informational keywords ("what is sterling silverware worth", "how much is my ring worth", "is it a good time to sell silver"). These attract browsers, not sellers.

### 4. Local SEO / GBP Signals
Alexandria dominates Local Pack results for "near me" queries. They likely have:
- Strong Google Business Profile optimization
- Consistent NAP across directories
- Location-specific content signals on their homepage
- Strong local backlink profile

Paradigm's Springfield location doesn't trigger for most "near me" queries in the DMV area.

## Key Observations

1. **Alexandria's brand name IS their keyword strategy.** "Alexandria Gold and Silver" naturally contains "gold and silver" — Google associates them with every "gold and silver" query. "Paradigm Experts" contains zero product keywords.

2. **Paradigm's content attracts curiosity, not sellers.** The antique tea set post (744 sessions) and sterling flatware value post (52 keywords) bring people researching items, not people ready to sell. No seller conversion path.

3. **No local landing pages.** Despite serving Arlington, McLean, Fairfax, Alexandria, DC — there's only a thin Springfield page. Alexandria doesn't need city pages because their brand name = their city.

4. **Service pages aren't optimized for search.** Pages like `/gold-gold-jewelry`, `/silver-flatware-hollowware`, `/diamonds-and-engagement-rings` rank for almost nothing. Poor URL structure, thin content, no local signals.

5. **HTTP/HTTPS split.** The homepage ranks at both `https://` and `http://` — canonical issue leaking authority.
