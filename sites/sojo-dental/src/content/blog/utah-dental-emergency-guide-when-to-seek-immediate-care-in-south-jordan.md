Dental emergencies don't wait for convenient times. Whether it's a weekend, holiday, or middle of the night, understanding when to seek immediate dental care can mean the difference between saving or losing a tooth. [The CDC reports that approximately 1 in 4 adults in the US have untreated tooth decay](https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/php/2024-oral-health-surveillance-report/selected-findings.html), which can lead to serious emergencies if left unaddressed.

## What Constitutes a True Dental Emergency?

Not every dental issue requires emergency treatment, but certain symptoms demand immediate attention. At Sojo Dental, we help South Jordan residents understand the difference between urgent care needs and situations that can wait for regular office hours.

### Immediate Emergency Situations:

- **Knocked-out permanent tooth:** Time is critical - reimplantation success drops significantly after 30 minutes
- **Severe, uncontrolled pain:** Often indicates infection or nerve exposure requiring prompt intervention
- **Facial swelling:** May signal serious infection that could spread to other areas
- **Uncontrolled bleeding:** Following trauma or oral surgery complications
- **Broken teeth with sharp fragments:** Risk of cutting tongue, lips, or cheeks

## Emergency Dental Care FAQ

### What should I do if my tooth gets knocked out?

Act quickly - time is critical for successful tooth reimplantation. First, locate the tooth and handle it only by the crown (top part), never the root. If dirty, gently rinse with milk or saline solution. Try to reinsert the tooth into its socket if possible, or store it in milk while heading to our South Jordan office immediately.

### How do I manage severe tooth pain at home?

Severe tooth pain often indicates infection or nerve damage requiring professional treatment. While waiting for emergency care, take over-the-counter pain medication as directed, apply a cold compress to reduce swelling, and avoid extremely hot or cold foods. Do not apply heat or place aspirin directly on the tooth, as this can cause tissue damage.

### When does facial swelling require emergency care?

Facial swelling accompanied by fever, difficulty swallowing, or breathing problems requires immediate medical attention. Even without these symptoms, significant facial swelling from dental infection can spread rapidly and should be evaluated within hours, not days.

### Can a cracked tooth wait until regular business hours?

Minor tooth cracks without pain can typically wait for regular office hours. However, if the crack causes sharp pain when biting, has rough edges cutting your tongue, or is accompanied by swelling, seek immediate care. Cover any sharp edges with sugar-free gum or dental wax temporarily.

[According to the American Dental Association, 100 million Americans fail to see a dentist each year](https://www.ada.org/resources/research/health-policy-institute), increasing their risk of emergency situations that could have been prevented with routine care.

Our team at Sojo Dental emphasizes preventive care to help South Jordan families avoid emergency situations. Regular checkups allow us to identify and treat problems before they become painful emergencies.
