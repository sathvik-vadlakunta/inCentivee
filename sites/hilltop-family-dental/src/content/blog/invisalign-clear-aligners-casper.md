If you've ever wished for straighter teeth but dreaded the idea of metal braces,

## How Invisalign works

Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners that gently shift your teeth into
place. You wear each set for about two weeks, then move to the next — gradually guiding
your smile to where it should be.

- **Virtually invisible** — most people won't even notice you're wearing them
- **Removable** — take them out to eat, brush, and floss as normal
- **Comfortable** — no metal brackets or wires to irritate your mouth
- **Predictable** — digital planning lets you preview your final smile before you start

## Who is a good candidate?

Invisalign works well for most common concerns — crowding, gaps, and many bite issues.
The best way to know if it's right for you is a quick consultation, where we'll scan your
teeth and map out your personalized treatment.

## How long does it take?

Most cases finish in 6–18 months, depending on complexity. We'll give you a clear
timeline up front, so there are no surprises.
