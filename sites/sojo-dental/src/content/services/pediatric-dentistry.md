SoJo Dental welcomes young patients of all ages. Dr. Conner Ogden and our team create a comfortable, positive experience that helps children develop healthy dental habits for life.

## Pediatric Services

- **Dental cleanings and exams** — age-appropriate preventive care
- **Digital X-rays** — low-radiation imaging for developing teeth
- **Fluoride treatments** — strengthen enamel and prevent cavities
- **Dental sealants** — protective coatings for cavity-prone molars
- **Pediatric fillings** — tooth-colored restorations for cavities
- **Pediatric crowns** — tooth-colored crowns for severely decayed baby teeth

## Our Approach

We understand that visiting the dentist can be intimidating for kids. Our team takes time to explain procedures in child-friendly language, uses gentle techniques, and creates a welcoming environment so your child feels safe and comfortable.

We recommend dental visits every six months starting at age 1 to establish good oral health habits early.
