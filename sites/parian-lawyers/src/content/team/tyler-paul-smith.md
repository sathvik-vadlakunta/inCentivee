Tyler Paul Smith is a skilled litigator ready to take on any case that calls for courtroom advocacy. Born and raised in Haralson County, Georgia — where he still lives on his family's 160-plus-year-old farm — Tyler brings a straightforward, plain-spoken approach to complex legal matters.

A graduate of The Citadel and John Marshall Law School in Atlanta, Tyler was elected to **Georgia's House of Representatives in 2020**, serving Carroll and Haralson Counties, where he has sponsored criminal-justice and district-focused legislation. His upbringing equips him to take complicated cases and boil them down into terms a jury understands.
