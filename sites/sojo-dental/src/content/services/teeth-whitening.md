A brighter smile can make a lasting impression. SoJo Dental offers professional teeth whitening solutions that deliver results far beyond what over-the-counter products can achieve.

## Our Whitening Options

### In-Office Whitening

Our in-office treatment uses professional-grade whitening agents to brighten your teeth in a single visit. The procedure takes approximately one hour and can lighten teeth by several shades.

### Take-Home Whitening Kits

We provide custom-fitted whitening trays with professional-strength gel for convenient at-home treatment. Most patients see full results within 1-2 weeks of daily use.

## Why Professional Whitening?

- **Stronger results** than store-bought strips or toothpastes
- **Custom-fitted trays** prevent gum irritation
- **Supervised treatment** minimizes sensitivity
- **Even, consistent whitening** across all teeth

According to the American Dental Association, [professional teeth whitening can brighten teeth by 3 to 8 shades in a single visit](https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/whitening), compared to just 1-2 shades with over-the-counter products.
