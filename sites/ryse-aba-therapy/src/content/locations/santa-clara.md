## Why In-Home ABA Works for Santa Clara

Santa Clara is a close-knit community within the St. George metropolitan area, served by the Washington County School District. For local families, in-home ABA removes a real barrier: there is no commute to a center, no waiting room, and no unfamiliar clinic. Therapy happens at your kitchen table, in the backyard, at the park, or wherever your child is most comfortable.

In-home care also lets parents and caregivers be part of sessions, so the strategies that work with your BCBA and therapy team continue after we leave. ABA is an evidence-based, individualized approach — not a cure, but a structured way to build meaningful skills at your child's pace.

Autism is more common than many families realize. According to the CDC's 2022 ADDM data, about [1 in 31 eight-year-old children in the U.S. were identified with autism](https://www.cdc.gov/autism/data-research/index.html), and Utah is one of the CDC's monitoring sites. You are not alone, and you do not have to wait to get help.
