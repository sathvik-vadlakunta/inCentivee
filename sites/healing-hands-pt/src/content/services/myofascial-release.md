## Free the fascia, free your movement

Myofascial release is a **hands-on engagement of the tissue** that allows tight, restricted fascia to let go. Depending on what your body needs, it can be **extremely gentle or very deep**.

## What it can do

- Reduce soreness and improve tissue recovery
- Increase blood flow
- Improve joint range of motion
- Decrease the physical effects of stress on the body

Myofascial release is one of Dr. Jamie's specialties and a frequent part of our hands-on, root-cause approach.
