After your dental implant has integrated with the jawbone, a custom restoration is attached to complete your smile. SoJo Dental provides a full range of implant-supported restorations.

## Types of Implant Restorations

### Single-Tooth Crowns
A custom porcelain crown attached to a single implant. Ideal for replacing one missing tooth with a result that looks and functions like a natural tooth.

### Implant-Supported Bridges
For patients missing several adjacent teeth, a bridge anchored by two or more implants eliminates the need for a removable partial denture.

### Implant-Supported Dentures
Full or partial dentures secured by implants for a stable, comfortable fit. No more slipping, adhesives, or dietary restrictions.

All restorations are custom-fabricated to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long do dental implant restorations last?**

With proper care, they can last a lifetime, making them a reliable and cost-effective choice.

**Do implants feel like natural teeth?**

Yes, once placed, dental implants look and function like your natural teeth.

**Can implant restorations help prevent shifting teeth?**

Absolutely. They help maintain spacing and alignment by filling in the gap left by a missing tooth.

**Is bone grafting always necessary before an implant?**

Not always. Our dentists will assess your jawbone and recommend treatment based on your needs.
