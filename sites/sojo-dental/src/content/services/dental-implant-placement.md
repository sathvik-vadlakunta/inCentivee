Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. At SoJo Dental, Dr. Brian Call places implants using advanced digital planning and guided surgery techniques for precise, predictable results.

## Why Choose Dental Implants?

- **Permanent solution** — implants fuse with your jawbone
- **Preserve bone** — prevent the bone loss that occurs with missing teeth
- **Natural function** — eat, speak, and smile with confidence
- **No impact on adjacent teeth** — unlike bridges, implants stand alone
- **High success rate** — over 95% success rate in healthy patients

## The Implant Process

1. **Consultation & planning** — 3D imaging and digital treatment planning
2. **Implant placement** — titanium post surgically placed in the jawbone
3. **Healing period** — 3-4 months for the implant to integrate with bone
4. **Restoration** — custom crown, bridge, or denture attached to the implant

IV sedation is available for implant procedures to ensure your complete comfort.
