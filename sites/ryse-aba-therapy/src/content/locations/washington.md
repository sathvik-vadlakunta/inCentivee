## In-Home ABA Therapy in Washington, Utah — No Waitlist

If you're a Washington family looking for ABA therapy, the hardest part is usually the wait. **At Ryse ABA Therapy, there is no waitlist — families start right away.** No months-long delay, no driving to a center across town. Our BCBA-led team brings personalized, play-based ABA therapy directly into your home and community, right here in Washington City and across Washington County.

Washington is one of Southern Utah's fastest-growing communities — the second-largest city in Washington County, with a population of nearly 28,000 as of the 2020 Census ([U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/washingtoncityutah/PST045224)). For growing families balancing school, work, and everyday life, in-home care that fits your schedule matters.

### ABA Services Available in Washington

Ryse provides individualized Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) for children, teens, and adults — **ages 2 through 65**. Most providers stop at kids; we support learners across the lifespan. Every plan is built around your family's real goals: communication, daily living and self-help skills, social connection, play, and reducing behaviors that get in the way of learning. Care is data-driven and personalized, supervised by our Clinical Director, **Noah Rasmussen, BCBA**.

### Why In-Home ABA Works for Washington Families

ABA therapy is most meaningful where life actually happens. When your child practices skills in their own home, neighborhood, and routines — getting ready in the morning, playing with a sibling, a trip to the store — those skills are more likely to stick. There's no clinic commute, no unfamiliar waiting room, and no rearranging your week around a center's hours. Students across Washington attend the Washington County School District; in-home ABA can complement what happens during the school day by building skills in the home environment too.

ABA is an evidence-based, individualized approach to supporting people with autism ([Behavior Analyst Certification Board](https://www.bacb.com/)). We practice person-first and affirm each learner's individuality — no guarantees, no cure language, just consistent, caring support built around your child.

### Insurance and No Waitlist — The Friction We Remove

We know insurance is confusing, so **we verify your benefits for you, free.** ABA therapy is commonly covered when there's an autism diagnosis and active coverage. In Utah, Medicaid covers medically necessary ABA services for eligible members with an ASD diagnosis ([Utah Medicaid — ASD Related Services](https://medicaid.utah.gov/ltc-2/asd/)). Eligibility honestly requires two things: an autism diagnosis and active coverage. From there, because we keep **no waitlist**, your family can begin within days.
