If the thought of a dental appointment makes your stomach tighten, you are far from alone. Dental anxiety is one of the most common reasons people put off care — and unfortunately, the longer care is delayed, the bigger small problems tend to become. The good news: **sedation dentistry** makes it possible to get the treatment you need calmly and comfortably. At **Oakridge Dental in Farmington**, we offer a full range of sedation options so no one has to choose between their comfort and their oral health.

## Why dental anxiety deserves to be taken seriously

Avoiding the dentist out of fear creates a difficult cycle: a small cavity that could've been a simple [filling](/services/dental-fillings) becomes a [root canal](/services/root-canal-therapy); a treatable gum issue progresses; and the anxiety only grows. Sedation breaks that cycle. It lets nervous patients get comfortable in the chair, get the care they need, and start fresh — often realizing the experience was far easier than they feared.

Sedation isn't only for anxiety, either. It can also help patients who:

- Have a **strong gag reflex**
- Have **difficulty getting numb** with local anesthetic alone
- Need **a lot of treatment** done in one visit
- Have a hard time **sitting still** for longer procedures
- Have **special healthcare needs**

### Nitrous oxide (laughing gas)

The mildest and most common option. You breathe a blend of nitrous oxide and oxygen through a small mask, and within minutes a gentle wave of calm settles in. You stay fully awake and aware, just relaxed. Because it wears off almost immediately when the mask comes off, **you can drive yourself home** — which makes it ideal for routine treatment and milder anxiety.

### Oral sedation

For moderate anxiety, we can prescribe a sedative you take by mouth before your appointment. By the time treatment begins, you'll feel deeply relaxed and drowsy — many patients remember little of the visit — while still being awake enough to respond. You'll need someone to drive you to and from your appointment.

### IV sedation

Delivered through a vein, IV sedation produces a deeper, carefully controlled level of relaxation. Most patients drift into a dream-like state and have little to no memory of the procedure afterward. It's an excellent choice for longer or more complex treatment, significant anxiety, or [oral surgery](/services/wisdom-teeth-removal). We monitor you closely throughout, and you'll need a trusted adult to drive you home.

### General anesthesia

The deepest level, in which you're fully asleep for the entire procedure. General anesthesia is reserved for the most involved treatments or for patients whose anxiety or special needs make other options impractical. As with IV sedation, you'll be monitored carefully and will need a ride home and the rest of the day to rest.

## Is sedation safe?

Yes — sedation dentistry has an excellent safety record when it's provided by a trained, experienced team following the [American Dental Association's Guidelines for the Use of Sedation and General Anesthesia by Dentists](https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/anesthesia-and-sedation). Before any sedation, we:

1. **Review your full health history** and current medications.
2. **Recommend the most appropriate, lowest-effective level** of sedation for your needs.
3. **Monitor you throughout** the appointment for your comfort and safety.
4. **Give you clear instructions** for before and after your visit.

We'll answer every question you have before we begin, so you feel informed and in control.

## Sedation for children

Dental visits can be overwhelming for very young or anxious children. We offer **pediatric-appropriate sedation**, including [pediatric nitrous oxide](/services/pediatric-nitrous-oxide) and [pediatric sedation](/services/pediatric-sedation), always carefully dosed for a child's size and closely monitored. For the right child, it can turn a stressful experience into a calm, manageable one — and help build a positive, lifelong relationship with the dentist.
