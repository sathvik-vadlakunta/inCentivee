If the thought of a dental appointment makes you tense up, you're not alone — and you're
always welcome at Hilltop Family Dentistry. Dental anxiety is one of the most common reasons
people put off care, and we've built our practice around making visits feel different.

## You're in very good company

Dental fear is genuinely widespread. A systematic review and meta-analysis estimates that
about **15% of adults experience dental anxiety**, with roughly **12% reporting high dental
fear** — and among people seeking emergency dental care, that figure climbs to nearly
**half**. It's one of the most common reasons people delay treatment. Our patients tell us
about it all the time, and our team is experienced, patient, and completely judgment-free
about it.

## Why avoiding the dentist backfires

Here's the hard truth about dental anxiety: avoidance makes things worse. Small, easy-to-fix
problems — a tiny cavity, early gum inflammation — quietly grow into bigger ones that need
more involved (and more expensive) treatment. That can deepen the anxiety, creating a cycle.
Breaking it early, with a team that *gets it*, is the kindest thing you can do for both your
smile and your peace of mind.

## How we keep you comfortable

We believe gentle, unhurried care is the best anxiety medicine. At every visit we:

- **Listen first** — tell us what worries you, and we'll go at your pace
- **Explain everything** — no surprises; you always know what's happening and why
- **Go gently** — modern, minimally invasive techniques mean less discomfort
- **Offer breaks** — there's no rushing; you stay in control the entire time

## Sedation options when you want extra help

For patients who'd like more help relaxing, we offer **nitrous oxide** ("laughing gas") — a
safe, gentle option that helps you feel calm and at ease during treatment. It wears off
within minutes once the mask comes off, so you can even drive yourself home. It's a great
fit for everything from a routine cleaning to more involved [general dentistry](/services/general-dentistry).

## Small touches that make a big difference

- A warm, welcoming front desk and a calm office
- Clear cost estimates up front — financial surprises cause anxiety too
- A team that remembers you and your history
- Plenty of time to ask questions, with no pressure
