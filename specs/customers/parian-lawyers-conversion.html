4 · ⚠️ Compliance — Georgia Bar advertising rules (P1, do not skip)

Legal advertising is regulated by the Georgia Rules of Professional Conduct (Rules 7.1–7.2). Getting this wrong can get the client sanctioned — it's not just an SEO nicety. Every item below is a build requirement, not optional polish. Have Parian's counsel sign off before go-live.

RequiredTrigger on their siteWhat we add
Contingency-fee disclaimerThey use "No Fee Unless We Win" prominentlyConspicuous disclaimer, equal prominence: "No fee unless we win" refers to attorney fees only; the client is usually responsible for court costs and case expenses.
Past-results disclaimerThey display case results / settlementsOn every results display: "Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome. Each case is different and must be judged on its own facts." Tie figures to specific factual/legal circumstances.
Testimonial handlingClient testimonials carouselUse only real, client-permissioned testimonials; disclose any paid/portrayed endorsement. Don't edit into misleading claims.
No prohibited superlativesSEO instinct = "best", "expert", "specialist"Avoid in titles, meta, schema, and copy. "Specialist/expert" implies board certification GA doesn't recognize for PI → ethics violation. Use "experienced", "dedicated", "focused on personal injury".
Site-wide legal disclaimer—Footer on every page: "This website is for general information only and is not legal advice. Contacting us does not create an attorney-client relationship." Plus "Attorney Advertising" label.
2-year record retentionRule 7.2Keep a copy of the published site/files for 2 years after last publish. (We snapshot each deploy anyway — formalize it for Parian.)
GA Rule 7.1 comment: statements about past results "could be misleading or deceptive… and may create an unjustified expectation" unless tied to specific facts. "No fee unless you win" requires the costs-vs-fees disclaimer. (See sources.)

Entity authority (the AEO unlock): link each attorney as a verified entity — sameAs to their State Bar of GA profile, Justia, Avvo, LinkedIn. AI engines trust lawyers they can verify against the bar. This is the legal equivalent of the dental "knowsAbout" play, but stricter.

6 · Content, tone & AEO

Tone — the opposite of cosmetic dentistry

PI prospects are scared, injured, and vulnerable. Aggressive "BOOK NOW" sales energy backfires. Lead with reassurance, empathy, and "here's what to do next" — then the firm's track record as quiet proof. Keep their voice ("Call Cade. Get Paid.") but wrap it in trust, not hype.

Practice-area pages (replaces "procedures")

One page per PI subtype (verify against live site): car accidents · truck accidents · motorcycle · pedestrian · bus · catastrophic injury · wrongful death · slip & fall / premises · nursing-home abuse · product liability / mass torts · dog bites · disability (ERISA / SSD / workers' comp / LTD). Each: empathetic intro → "what to do after a ___" → how we help → case-value factors → 3–5 FAQs (FAQPage schema) → area-served line → disclaimered results.

AEO — answer the real questions people ask AI

AI Overviews now overlap traditional top-10 only ~17–38% (down from 76%) — being citable matters as much as ranking. Build question-first content + FAQ schema around the queries PI clients actually ask assistants:

  • "How much is my car accident case worth in Georgia?"
  • "How long do I have to file a personal injury claim in Georgia?" (statute of limitations)
  • "What should I do after a truck accident?"
  • "Do I need a lawyer for a slip and fall?"

Answer concisely up top, cite real sources (GA Code, court data, bar guidance — never fabricate figures), attribute to a named attorney.

  • Secondary: "Law Firm", "Trial Attorney".
  • Fill every service (car/truck/wrongful-death…), full 750-char description, real photos, posts, Q&A.
  • 3 separate GBP listings (Carrollton, Bremen, Atlanta) — each its own NAP, each verified.

Directories (legal vertical)

Use VERTICAL_DIRECTORIES["legal"] in directory_profiles.py (already defined): Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Lawyers.com, Martindale-Hubbell, Super Lawyers, Nolo, LegalMatch, State Bar of Georgia directory. Plus universal (GBP, Apple, Bing, Yelp, BBB, Chamber). Drop the dental set (Healthgrades, Zocdoc, ADA).

Multi-office NAP (harder than single-location dental)

Three offices = three NAPs that must stay perfectly consistent across the site, schema, and every directory. A location page per office (with embedded map, local content, the office's own GBP link). NAP inconsistency across 3 locations is the easiest way to tank the map pack — citation cleanup is bigger here than a one-office dental practice.
