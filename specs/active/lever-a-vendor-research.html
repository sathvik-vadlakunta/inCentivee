SPEC · VENDOR RESEARCH

Lever A — Digital-PR / Earned-Mention Vendors

A research + pricing framework to evaluate every option for buying off-site brand mentions (the #1 AI-visibility lever) per customer, and pick the best fit for the ~$3k/mo, turnkey, white-label model. Fill the matrix from vendor calls, score, and pilot.

Owner: Kody · Status: Research spec · 2026-06-17 · pricing = public market data (confirm with each vendor; most quote privately).

What we're buying (one sentence)

Get each customer named/quoted on reputable third-party sites that AI engines cite — delivered under PracticeRank's brand, priced per outcome (placement), not as an open-ended retainer.

Our fit criteria: per-placement/à-la-carte (not retainer) · white-label · turnkey (no VA) · AI-citation relevant (real editorial/news/directory, not spammy links) · margin-safe at ~$200–350/customer/mo · ideally an order API + delivers placement URLs we can tie to "sources now citing you".

💡 The cheap path (recommended first) — DIY HARO + automate

Paid placements ($200–350 ea) are the lazy/fast way. The near-free way: respond to free journalist requests, and automate the tedious part so it scales.

Timelines

  • HARO: ~30% of picked pitches publish within 1 week, ~50% within 2 weeks; 10–20% success per pitch → 3–5 mentions/mo with consistent pitching. Pitch within the first 6 hours (ideally minutes) for far higher odds — why automation/speed matters.
  • Paid per-placement: ~2–4 weeks turnaround each.
  • AI citation pickup: days–weeks after a placement is published + indexed → meaningful lift over 4–8 weeks (matches the Score-v2 "progress in 4–6 weeks" promise).

Questions to ask every vendor

1. Exact wholesale/reseller price per placement, and at what volume tiers?
2. Is it per-placement or a monthly minimum/retainer per client?
3. Can you show 5–10 sample live placements (so we can check the sites are real, indexed, and the kind AI cites)?
4. Fully white-label — nothing branded to you reaches our client?
5. Do you draft + outreach + place end-to-end, or do we supply content?
6. Is there an API or bulk portal to submit orders + get back the placement URL/date?
7. Turnaround per placement? Min term / cancellation?
8. Can you land local/regional + niche outlets (e.g., precious-metals, dental, legal)?
9. Do you guarantee placement, or is it best-effort (HARO-style)? Refund/replace policy?
10. Any work that would put our clients at Google-penalty risk (PBNs, paid-link footprints)?

Recommended process

  1. Send the 10 questions to: Reporter Outreach, RankZ, The AEO Collective, E2M, + 1 HARO-response service.
  2. Fill the matrix; score with the rubric.
  3. Pilot the top 2 on one real customer each (e.g., Paradigm + a dental client) — 1 placement; verify it appears in our "sources now citing you" within 2–4 weeks.
  4. Pick the winner; wire the automated handoff (auto-brief → auto-order → attribution) per the ops plan.
