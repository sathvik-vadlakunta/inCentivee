# Google AI Overviews Changed Local SEO Forever — Most Dentists Haven't Noticed

For years, the goal was simple: get your dental practice to page 1 of Google. Ideally position 1-3. Run some ads. Optimize your Google Business Profile. Watch the phone ring.

That playbook still works. But something appeared above all of it — and it's quietly redirecting patient attention before they ever scroll to your listing.

**Google AI Overviews.**

That AI-generated summary at the top of Google search results now appears on [up to 60% of all search queries](https://xponent21.com/insights/google-ai-overviews-surpass-60-percent/), including dental and healthcare searches. And it's changing everything about how patients find a dentist.

## What Patients See Now

When a patient searches "best dentist for veneers near me," here's what they encounter (from top to bottom):

1. **Google AI Overview** — An AI-generated paragraph answering their question, often naming specific practices
2. **Sponsored ads** — Paid placements
3. **Local map pack** — The top 3 Google Business Profile listings
4. **Organic results** — Traditional blue links

The AI Overview sits above everything. It gets the first look, the first read, and increasingly, the first click (or no click at all — because the AI already answered the question).

If your practice is cited in the AI Overview, you win attention before the patient ever sees the map pack or organic results. If you're not cited, you're competing for leftover attention below an answer the patient may have already accepted.

## The Zero-Click Problem

Data shows that AI Overviews are accelerating "zero-click searches" — queries where the user gets their answer without clicking any result. [65% of all Google searches in 2026 now end without a click](https://click-vision.com/zero-click-search-statistics), and [for queries where an AI Overview appears, 83% result in zero clicks](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/zero-click-search-2026-ai-overviews-data-update). For informational queries, this has been happening for years with featured snippets.

But now it's happening for **local service queries.** When a patient asks about dental services and the AI Overview recommends a practice by name, the vast majority of users take that recommendation without scrolling further.

Your SEO ranking hasn't changed. Your Google Business Profile is still optimized. But the patient never sees either one because the AI Overview answered their question first.

## Why This Is Different from Featured Snippets

Featured snippets pulled content from a single source. If you ranked well, you could "earn" the snippet.

AI Overviews are different. They synthesize information from multiple sources, apply their own logic, and generate a unique response. You can't simply optimize a single page to appear in the AI Overview. The AI is making a judgment call about which practices to mention based on a complex set of signals.

This means practices with strong, multi-source authority get cited. Practices with a single well-optimized website — but weak presence elsewhere — often don't.

## What Google AI Overviews Value

Based on patterns we've observed across thousands of AI-generated responses in healthcare, Google's AI Overviews appear to prioritize:

**Consistent entity information.** Your practice name, address, specialty, and credentials must be consistent and verified across multiple platforms — not just your website and GBP.

**Professional depth.** The AI cites practices that have demonstrable expertise in the specific service being searched. Generic "We do everything!" practices get passed over in favor of practices with clear specialty authority.

**Patient validation.** Reviews play a role, but not just the star count. The AI appears to weigh the substance of reviews — do patients mention specific procedures, outcomes, and experiences? Generic 5-star reviews carry less signal than detailed ones.

**Freshness.** Stale information reduces AI confidence. Practices with recent activity — recent reviews, recent content, recently updated business profiles — signal that they're active and current.

## The Double Standard for Healthcare

Google holds healthcare queries to a higher standard than other categories. This is the E-E-A-T framework on steroids — for medical and dental queries, the AI is especially cautious about who it recommends.

This means:

- Credential signals matter more (DDS, DMD, board certifications)
- Professional affiliations get weighted (ADA membership, state associations)
- Content must demonstrate first-hand expertise
- Unverified claims are filtered aggressively

This actually creates an opportunity for legitimate dental practices. The bar for AI citation in healthcare is high enough that spammy competitors and generic directory sites get filtered out. If you can meet that bar, you're competing against a smaller, more qualified field.

## The Local SEO Stack Is Now Three Layers

The old local SEO stack was two layers:

1. Google Ads (pay to play)
2. Organic + Maps (earn through SEO)

Now it's three:

1. **AI Overviews** (earn through authority and structured signals)
2. Google Ads (pay to play)
3. Organic + Maps (earn through SEO)

Layer 1 is the newest, the least understood, and the highest-impact per impression. It's also where the least competition exists right now.

Practices investing in all three layers will capture patients at every level of the search experience. Practices only investing in layers 2 and 3 are leaving the highest-value real estate empty — and their competitors will fill it.

## What This Means for Your Marketing Budget

If you're spending money on Google Ads and traditional SEO but not optimizing for AI search (both Google's AI Overviews and third-party AI engines), you're investing in the bottom two layers while ignoring the top.

This isn't an argument to stop SEO or ads. It's an argument to **add AI visibility to the stack** before your competitors do.

The practices that are visible across all three layers — AI Overviews, ads, and organic — will dominate patient acquisition in their markets. The window for building that position affordably is now.
