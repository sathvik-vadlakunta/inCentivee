For patients who prefer a metal-free approach to dental restoration, SoJo Dental offers ceramic (zirconia) dental implants. These biocompatible implants provide the same strength and function as titanium with an all-white, natural appearance.

## Benefits of Metal-Free Implants

- **Biocompatible** — ideal for patients with metal sensitivities or allergies
- **Natural aesthetics** — white ceramic material eliminates any gray showing through gums
- **Corrosion-resistant** — no metallic taste or galvanic reactions
- **Strong and durable** — zirconia offers excellent strength and bone integration
- **Holistic option** — popular with patients who prefer metal-free healthcare

## Who Are They For?

Metal-free implants are a great choice for patients with metal allergies, those who prefer holistic dentistry, or anyone seeking the most natural-looking result — especially in the front of the mouth where aesthetics matter most.

Dr. Brian Call evaluates each patient to determine whether ceramic or titanium implants are the best option for their specific needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What makes zirconia different from metal implants?**

Zirconia is non-metallic, white and hypoallergenic, offering aesthetic and health advantages over traditional titanium implants.

**Are metal-free implants as strong as metal ones?**

Yes, zirconia is extremely durable and offers excellent strength for everyday use.

**How do I care for metal-free implants?**

Care is similar to natural teeth – daily brushing, flossing and routine dental visits are essential.

**Can I receive metal-free implants with minimal bone density?**

Yes, zirconia implants often require less bone than traditional options, though a consultation will confirm your eligibility.

**Do you serve South Jordan, Utah?**

Yes, we proudly provide metal-free dental implants in South Jordan, Utah, and for the surrounding areas of Herriman, Riverton and West Jordan, Utah.
