# DentalRank.ai — Website Redesign & Copy Overhaul

## Current State Analysis

### What exists today
A single-page site with:
- Hero: "Your patients are searching. Who are they finding?"
- Free report lead magnet (website URL + email form)
- Stats section (87%, 10+, 100% free)
- "What You'll Get" — report preview with score mockup
- "What Actually Moves the Needle" — G / AI / L badges
- Testimonial (anonymous: "Practice Owner, Austin TX")
- How It Works (3 steps)
- FAQ (6 questions)
- Footer (just copyright + privacy)

### What's wrong

**1. No clear value proposition — what do you SELL?**
The entire site positions DentalRank as a free audit tool. There is zero mention of the actual service: a $3,000/month done-for-you dental marketing platform. A visitor who reads the whole page thinks you're a free report generator, not a marketing agency. There's no pricing page, no services page, no "what we do" page.

**2. CTA is weak — "Get Your Free Report" is a dead end**
The only action is getting a free report. There is no path to book a sales call, no calendar link, no "Talk to us" button, no phone number. The line "No sales calls" actively discourages the conversion you want.

**3. No credibility signals**
- The one testimonial is anonymous ("Practice Owner, Austin TX") — might as well not exist
- No logos, no case studies, no before/after results
- No team page — who is behind DentalRank?
- No mention of how many practices you work with

**4. No services breakdown**
The site mentions GBP, AI search, and local authority in vague terms but never explains what DentalRank actually does for clients. The new capabilities (llms.txt, schema markup, Grade.us reviews, referral programs, content publishing, Apple Maps) are completely absent.

**5. No differentiation**
Every dental SEO agency says "we help you rank." The site doesn't explain WHY DentalRank is different — the AI-powered automation, the 85% hands-off model, the GEO/AEO focus that competitors don't offer.

**6. No urgency or scarcity**
Nothing creates FOMO or a reason to act now. The "AI search window" is mentioned in the FAQ but buried.

**7. Design is data-light**
The site uses placeholder-feeling visuals (styled divs, letter badges). No screenshots of real reports, no before/after dashboards, no real data visualizations.

## Key Copy Principles for the Rewrite

1. **Lead with the AI search angle** — this is the differentiator. Every dental SEO agency talks about Google rankings. Nobody is talking about ChatGPT/Perplexity visibility to dental practices.

2. **Show, don't tell** — mockup screenshots of a practice appearing in ChatGPT results are worth 1,000 words of copy.

3. **Sell the outcome, not the tool** — "More patients finding you" not "We deploy llms.txt files." Patients don't know what schema markup is. They know what "getting more calls" means.

4. **Make the CTA a conversation, not a commitment** — "Book a free 20-minute strategy call" is better than "Get started" or "Sign up." Low friction, high value.

5. **Kill "No sales calls"** — this was meant to reduce friction for the free report, but it signals that talking to you has no value. Replace with "We'll walk you through your report together — no pressure, no pitch."

6. **Use specific numbers everywhere** — "$3K/month" is better than "affordable." "22% more AI citations" is better than "improved visibility." "2.3x more calls" is better than "better results."

7. **Create urgency around the AI window** — "AI search can't be bought yet. First movers will own these results." This is the one thing that creates genuine FOMO because it's true.
