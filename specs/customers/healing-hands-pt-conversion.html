3.5 · The CORE positioning fix the whole point

The owner's real problem isn't her care — it's articulating why a patient should pay out of pocket for it. Here's the message, grounded in how the best cash-pay manual-therapy clinics position it and sharpened with Hormozi's value equation.

The one-sentence positioning

"At a typical insurance clinic, your therapist sees 3 patients an hour and you spend most of the visit on machines and a handout. Here, you get a full hour, one-on-one, hands-on with a doctor — so we fix the root cause in fewer visits, not chase the symptom forever."

The contrast table (use this verbatim as a homepage section)

Typical insurance PTHealing Hands (concierge, cash-pay)
Time with the doctor~15–20 min, shared 2–4 patients/hourA full 60 min, 1:1, every visit
Who treats youOften an aide / tech for much of the visitDr. Jamie (PT, DPT, MTC) — the whole time
What you doRepetitive gym exercises & machinesHands-on manual therapy first, then targeted home exercises
Who decides your careInsurance "medical necessity" rulesYou and your doctor
Visit cadence2–3 visits/week for weeksFewer, longer visits — often 6–10 total
Cost realityCopays + deductible + possible denied claimsKnown up front; superbill for out-of-network reimbursement

Grounded in real cash-pay-PT positioning (Austin Manual Therapy, SPRY, Healix, Nashville PT): "2–3, sometimes 4 patients in a single hour" → "15–20 min per patient"; "6–10 sessions annually vs 2–3 weekly"; "most patients receive 50–80% out-of-network reimbursement"; treatment "based on your condition, not what an insurance plan deems 'medically necessary'." (See §10 sources.)

Hormozi value equation applied to the cash-pay PT offer

Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

LeverHow we push it on the new site
↑ Dream outcomeConcrete, visualizable: "back to golf / pickleball / running / playing with the kids — without surgery or injections." Use Dr. Jamie's real wins (avoided knee replacement; off pain meds).
↑ Perceived likelihoodCredentials (DPT + MTC, 15 yrs), Best-of-Sierra-Nevada award, real named testimonials with conditions, root-cause explanation, Google rating (live).
↓ Time delay"Relief often in the first visit" (Jeff's testimonial), "fewer total visits," easy call/text booking, mobile visits that remove the commute.
↓ Effort & sacrifice"No driving across town to a clinic 3× a week," superbill done for you, direct phone/text line to your doctor, transparent pricing, risk-reversal guarantee (below).

The Grand-Slam offer + guarantee (Phase-2 conversion content)

Hormozi catalogs four guarantee types: unconditional, conditional, anti-, and implied. For a licensed healthcare provider, an outcome guarantee is legally/ethically fraught — so use a conditional, experience-based guarantee (risk-reversal on the experience, never a clinical outcome):

"The First-Visit Promise:" On your first visit you get a full hour with Dr. Jamie, a hands-on root-cause evaluation, and a clear plan. If you don't feel heard and confident in your plan by the end of that hour, tell us — we'll make it right. (Frames the experience, not a medical result. Run final wording past the client / NV PT board guidance.)

Value stack to display (each line is something insurance PT doesn't give): full 60-min 1:1 doctor visit · hands-on manual therapy + dry needling / CST / StemWave as needed · gait/posture/mobility root-cause eval · personalized home program · direct phone/text line to your doctor · superbill prepared for you · mobile visit option · no insurance gatekeeping. ⚠️ Confirm real session pricing with the client before publishing any numbers — never fabricate a price.

5 · Phase 2 — modern redesign direction after review

The client wants it "way more modern." Target: a premium, calm, clinical-but-human aesthetic — the visual equivalent of an unhurried 60-minute hands-on session. Not a funnel; a boutique health brand.

Visual direction

  • Hero: full-bleed real photo of Dr. Jamie's hands at work (or the calm treatment room), with the positioning headline overlaid — lead with "A full hour, one-on-one. Hands-on. Not dictated by insurance."
  • Palette: Healing Hands logo purple (#a26fa8, deepened to #8a4a92 for contrast) + warm mauve-grey on lots of soft white + a warm off-white "bone/sand" canvas; generous whitespace; rounded 2xl cards; soft shadows (already in the system).
  • Type: keep Nunito (friendly, rounded) for display; Open Sans for body. Larger, confident H1; airy line-height.
  • Motion: gentle scroll-reveal + subtle parallax (already built, reduced-motion-safe). Nothing flashy — calm signals premium.
  • Photography: real, warm, human (hands, faces, the room) — replace all gradient placeholders once the asset gate clears.

Homepage section order (conversion-first)

  1. Hero — positioning headline + "Book your appointment" / call-text
  2. Insurance-PT vs Healing Hands contrast table (§3.5) — the hook
  3. Before→after story band (Jeff / Judy — pain → relief, with the real quote)
  4. Services grid (9, real)
  5. "Our care is not dictated by insurance" — the cash-pay value, reframed as benefit
  6. Value stack + First-Visit Promise guarantee card
  7. Conditions treated
  8. Testimonial wall + live Google rating
  9. Meet Dr. Jamie (credibility) → FAQ (AEO) → final CTA

Trust / credibility layer to add

  • Live Google rating badge + AggregateRating (pull via Places API with the captured Place ID; scripts/fetch-reviews.mjs pattern).
  • Credential strip: DPT · MTC · 15+ years · Best of Sierra Nevada (Bronze).
  • "Dry needling is NOT acupuncture" explainer (their most-asked question → strong AEO + objection handler).
  • Sticky mobile call/book bar (already in the layout) + click-to-call / form events once analytics is wired.
