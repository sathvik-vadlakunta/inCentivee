No more temporary crowns and multiple visits. SoJo Dental offers CEREC same-day crowns that are designed, milled, and placed in a single appointment.

## How Same-Day Crowns Work

1. **Digital scan** — A 3D digital impression replaces messy molds
2. **Computer design** — Your crown is designed on-screen for a perfect fit
3. **In-office milling** — A ceramic block is precision-milled into your custom crown
4. **Bonding** — The crown is bonded to your tooth and adjusted for a comfortable bite

## Benefits

- One appointment instead of two
- No temporary crown or return visit
- Precision digital fit
- Natural-looking ceramic material
- Same-day convenience
