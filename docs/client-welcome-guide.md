# Part 2: Platform Access

We need access to a few platforms to manage your online presence. Each one takes about 2 minutes. If you get stuck on any step, we are happy to walk you through it on a quick call.

## 1. Google Business Profile

Grant us **Manager** access so we can optimize your listing, respond to reviews, and post updates.

1. Go to your Google Business Profile (search your practice name on Google, click "Edit profile")
2. Click **People and access**
3. Click **Add user**
4. Enter: **jonlucas@lostrelic.com**
5. Set role to **Manager**
6. Click **Invite**

## 2. Google Search Console

This gives us access to your real search data so we can see exactly what patients are searching for.

1. Go to **search.google.com/search-console**
2. Select your property
3. Click **Settings** (gear icon) in the left sidebar
4. Click **Users and permissions**
5. Click **Add user**
6. Enter: **jonlucas@lostrelic.com**
7. Set permission to **Full**
8. Click **Add**

## 3. Google Analytics

We use this to track how visitors interact with your website and measure results.

1. Go to **analytics.google.com**
2. Click **Admin** (gear icon, bottom left)
3. Click **Property Access Management**
4. Click the **+** button, then **Add users**
5. Enter: **jonlucas@lostrelic.com**
6. Check **Editor** role
7. Click **Add**

## 4. Google Tag Manager

This lets us add tracking and conversion measurement to your site.

1. Go to **tagmanager.google.com**
2. Click **Admin** in the top menu
3. Click **User Management**
4. Click the **+** button, then **Add users**
5. Enter: **jonlucas@lostrelic.com**
6. Set access to **Publish**
7. Click **Invite**

## 5. Webflow (Your Website)

We need three things from Webflow: collaborator access, an API token, and your Site ID.

### Step A: Add Us as a Collaborator

1. Log in to **webflow.com**
2. Click on your site to open the Dashboard
3. Go to **Site Settings** (gear icon in the left sidebar)
4. Click **Members** in the left menu
5. Click **Invite Collaborator**
6. Enter: **jonlucas@lostrelic.com**
7. Set role to **Can Edit**
8. Click **Send Invite**

### Step B: Generate an API Token

We use this to automatically publish SEO improvements like schema markup, meta tags, and blog posts. It only works for your specific site.

1. In **Site Settings**, click **Apps & Integrations** in the left menu
2. Scroll down to **API Access**
3. Click **Generate API Token**
4. Name it: **PracticeRank**
5. Turn on these permissions:
   - **Sites** - Read and Write
   - **Pages** - Read and Write
   - **Custom Code** - Read and Write
   - **CMS** - Read and Write
6. Click **Generate Token**
7. **Copy the token right away** - Webflow only shows it once

**Important:** Please do not send the API token over email. Instead:

- Share it via a password manager (1Password, LastPass)
- Use **onetimesecret.com** - paste the token, then send us the link
- Or read it to us on a phone call

### Step C: Send Us Your Site ID

1. In **Site Settings**, click **General**
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Copy the **Site ID** (it looks like `site_abc123def456`)
4. Email it to jonlucas@lostrelic.com (this one is fine to send over email)

## 6. DNS / Domain Access

We move your DNS to Cloudflare (free) for faster page loads, better security, and to serve SEO files. This does not change your hosting or website.

Please share one of the following:

- Login credentials for your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains, etc.)
- Or add us as a delegate/authorized user on the account

## 7. Apple Business (If Applicable)

If your practice is listed on Apple Maps, we can optimize it for Siri and Apple Maps searches.

1. Go to **business.apple.com**
2. Sign in with your Apple ID
3. If your listing is not yet claimed, follow the prompts to claim it
4. Once claimed, go to **Users**
5. Add **jonlucas@lostrelic.com** as an **Admin**

If your listing is not claimed yet, no problem. Just let us know and we will help you through it.
