A single-tooth dental implant is the ideal solution for replacing one missing tooth without affecting the teeth on either side. Unlike a bridge, which requires grinding down adjacent teeth, an implant stands alone as a permanent, independent restoration.

## How It Works

1. A titanium or ceramic implant post is placed in the jawbone
2. The implant integrates with the bone over 3-4 months
3. A custom porcelain crown is attached to the implant

The result looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural tooth.

## Advantages Over a Bridge

- **Preserves healthy teeth** — no alteration of adjacent teeth required
- **Prevents bone loss** — the implant stimulates the jawbone just like a natural root
- **Easy to clean** — brush and floss normally, just like your other teeth
- **Long-lasting** — with proper care, a single implant can last a lifetime

Our team uses digital planning and guided placement for precise, predictable results.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does a single-tooth dental implant last?**

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a reliable long-term solution.

**Can I eat normally with an implant?**

Yes, once fully healed, the implant functions like a natural tooth and allows you to enjoy your favorite foods again.

**What is the healing time for an implant?**

Initial healing may take a few days, but full integration of the implant typically takes three to six months.

**Do I need bone grafting for a single-tooth implant?**

This depends on your jawbone density. Our dentists will evaluate this during your initial consultation.

Yes, we offer single-tooth dental implants in South Jordan, Utah, and for the surrounding areas of Herriman, Riverton and West Jordan, Utah.
