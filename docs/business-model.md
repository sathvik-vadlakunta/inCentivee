# Business Model & Unit Economics

## Pricing

- **$3,000/month** flat fee per practice
- No setup fees, no long-term contracts
- 90-day results-or-refund guarantee: if the PracticeRank Score doesn't improve 20+ points in 90 days, full refund (client keeps everything built) — this is the public-facing guarantee; keep it consistent everywhere
- Exclusivity: one practice per specialty per zip code (creates urgency)
- Founding partner pricing: $1,500-2,000/mo for first 10 clients (6-month discount)

## The ROI Pitch

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Avg new patient lifetime value | $1,200-1,500 (first year) |
| Patients needed to cover $3k/mo | 2-3 |
| Typical delivery | 10-25+ new patients/month |
| Net ROI | 3-8x return on spend |

## Competitive Moat

1. **Distribution** — Founder's existing dental network (warm intros, not cold outreach)
2. **GEO first-mover** — First to offer AI search optimization for dental
3. **Data network effects** — 50+ practices generate unique performance data
4. **Switching costs** — PMS integration + GEO content + ad optimization = painful to rebuild

## Sales Strategy

- Warm outreach to founder's dental network ("want your input" not "buy my thing")
- First 10 as "founding partners" at discounted rate
- Case study machine: real numbers from first 5-10 clients
- Peer referral: $500/mo discount per converted referral
- Conference speaking: "How AI is changing patient acquisition"

## Objection Handling

- "Already have marketing company" → "Are they doing AI search? 40% of future patients will find you through ChatGPT first."
- "$3k is too expensive" → "Just 2-3 new patients covers it. We deliver 10-25+."
- "How different from Birdeye/Podium?" → "They manage reviews. We manage your entire acquisition engine + GEO."
- "Need to think about it" → "One practice per zip code. Your competitor signs up, that slot's gone."
