## One-on-one care that honors your time

Recovery should be a partnership. At Healing Hands Physical Therapy and Bodywork, we work closely with you to build a customized treatment plan focused on **your** goals. From your first consultation to your final session, you receive the undivided attention of a skilled, manual-therapy-trained doctor.

Whether you're recovering from surgery, managing a chronic condition, or overcoming a recent injury, our concierge model gives us the time to **find the root cause of your pain** — not just chase symptoms.

## What's included

- A **full hour**, one-on-one with your doctor — every visit
- A thorough evaluation of your **gait, posture, and mobility**
- Hands-on manual therapy and the right modalities for your case
- A personalized home program so you keep your progress
- A direct line to your doctor by phone, text, or email

## Our approach

We use a holistic approach that treats the whole person — body, mind, and soul. Instead of repetitive gym exercises several times a week, we focus on **bodywork first** so you get relief from pain and lasting, long-term results — and so you can get back to gardening, pickleball, running, yoga, golf, or playing with the kids.
