Your smile is one of the first things people notice. If chips, gaps, stains, or worn teeth are holding you back, cosmetic dentistry at Hilltop can help you smile with confidence again.

## Ways we enhance your smile

- **Porcelain veneers** — thin, custom shells that transform the shape, color, and alignment of your teeth
- **Cosmetic bonding** — quick, affordable repairs for chips, gaps, and small imperfections
- **Teeth whitening** — professional brightening that lifts years of stains
- **Smile makeovers** — a personalized combination of treatments to redesign your smile

## Designed to look natural — never fake

We plan every cosmetic case around your facial features and your goals, so the result enhances your smile without looking artificial. During your consultation, we'll show you your options and build a plan that fits your budget — with Cherry financing available.

## Built on healthy foundations

Beautiful results last when they're built on a healthy mouth. We make sure your teeth and gums are healthy first, then design a smile that's as durable as it is attractive.
