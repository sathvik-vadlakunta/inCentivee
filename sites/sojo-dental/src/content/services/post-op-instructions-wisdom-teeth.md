Following your wisdom teeth removal at SoJo Dental, proper aftercare is essential for a smooth recovery. Below are detailed instructions to help you heal comfortably.

## First 24 Hours

- **Bite on gauze** for 30-45 minutes after surgery to control bleeding
- **Apply ice packs** to the outside of your cheeks — 20 minutes on, 20 minutes off
- **Rest** with your head elevated; avoid strenuous activity
- **Eat soft foods** — yogurt, applesauce, mashed potatoes, smoothies (no straws)
- **Do not** rinse, spit forcefully, or use a straw — this can dislodge the blood clot

## Days 2-3

- Begin gentle salt water rinses (1/2 teaspoon salt in 8 oz warm water) after meals
- Continue soft foods; gradually reintroduce firmer foods as comfort allows
- Take prescribed medications as directed for pain management
- Swelling typically peaks on days 2-3 and gradually improves

## Days 4-7

- Most patients return to normal activities within 3-5 days
- Continue gentle oral hygiene, avoiding the surgical sites with your toothbrush
- If you notice increased pain after day 3, contact our office as this may indicate dry socket

## When to Call Us

Contact SoJo Dental at (801) 260-9150 if you experience:
- Excessive bleeding that doesn't subside with gauze pressure
- Fever above 101°F
- Severe pain not relieved by prescribed medication
- Numbness that persists beyond 24 hours
- Difficulty swallowing or breathing

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I brush my teeth after wisdom teeth removal?**

Yes, but avoid the surgical site. Brush gently and wait 24 hours before rinsing.

**When can I resume physical activity?**

Light activity is safe after 24 to 48 hours but avoid vigorous exercise until fully healed.

**Are there foods I should completely avoid?**

Yes, avoid crunchy, spicy, acidic and hot foods until the area has healed completely.

**How do I know if something is wrong during recovery?**

Excessive pain, swelling or fever may signal a problem. Call our office immediately if these occur.
