Dental anxiety is far more common than many people realize. [The 2021 Adult Oral Health Survey found that approximately 42% of adults experience moderate dental anxiety, with 12% experiencing extreme fear](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41415-024-7846-1). At Sojo Dental, we understand that this anxiety can prevent people from getting the dental care they need, which is why we've made anxiety management a cornerstone of our South Jordan practice.

## Understanding Dental Anxiety in 2026

Dental anxiety can stem from various sources - past traumatic experiences, fear of pain, feeling of helplessness, or even embarrassment about oral health. What many patients don't realize is that avoiding dental care due to anxiety often makes oral health problems worse. [The CDC reports that approximately 1 in 4 adults in the US have untreated tooth decay](https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/php/2024-oral-health-surveillance-report/selected-findings.html), and dental anxiety is often a contributing factor.

Our team at Sojo Dental recognizes that each patient's anxiety level is different. Some patients feel nervous about routine cleanings, while others avoid the dentist for years despite experiencing pain. We've developed a comprehensive approach to help patients at every anxiety level receive comfortable, quality dental care.

## IV Sedation: The Gold Standard for Dental Anxiety

For patients with moderate to severe dental anxiety, IV sedation represents one of the most effective solutions available in 2026. At Sojo Dental, we offer IV sedation as part of our commitment to making dental care accessible to all South Jordan residents, regardless of their anxiety level.

IV sedation works by delivering anti-anxiety medications directly into the bloodstream, creating a deeply relaxed state while allowing patients to remain conscious and responsive. Unlike general anesthesia, patients can still follow simple instructions and maintain their protective reflexes, making it a safer option for most dental procedures.

### Benefits of IV Sedation at Sojo Dental

- Immediate onset and precise control of sedation level
- Amnesia effect - patients often don't remember the procedure
- Ability to complete multiple treatments in one visit
- Minimal side effects with quick recovery
- Safe for most patients when administered by trained professionals

## Other Anxiety Management Techniques

While IV sedation is highly effective, our South Jordan practice also offers other anxiety management approaches:

### Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

A mild sedative that helps patients relax while remaining fully conscious. It's particularly effective for patients with mild to moderate anxiety and wears off quickly after treatment.

### Oral Sedation

Prescribed medications taken before the appointment to reduce anxiety. This option is convenient and effective for many patients, though the onset and effects are less predictable than IV sedation.

### Communication and Environment

Our team focuses on clear communication, explaining each step of procedures, and creating a calming environment. Sometimes, understanding what to expect can significantly reduce anxiety.

## The Cost of Avoiding Dental Care

Many anxious patients postpone dental treatment, but this avoidance often leads to more complex and expensive problems. [According to the American Dental Association, 100 million Americans fail to see a dentist each year](https://www.ada.org/resources/research/health-policy-institute), often due to anxiety or fear.

When dental problems are left untreated, they typically worsen over time. A simple filling might become a root canal, or a root canal might require tooth extraction and replacement. At Sojo Dental, we've seen how early intervention with appropriate anxiety management saves patients both discomfort and money in the long term.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Sedation

### Is IV sedation safe for dental procedures?

Yes, IV sedation is very safe when administered by trained dental professionals. At Sojo Dental, our team has extensive experience with IV sedation, and we monitor patients throughout the entire procedure to ensure their safety and comfort.

### Will I be unconscious during IV sedation?

No, you'll remain conscious during IV sedation but in a deeply relaxed state. You'll be able to respond to instructions and maintain protective reflexes, but most patients don't remember the procedure afterward due to the amnesia effect.

### How long does it take to recover from IV sedation?

Most patients feel alert within 2-4 hours after IV sedation, though we recommend taking the rest of the day easy. You'll need someone to drive you home and should avoid making important decisions for 24 hours.
