Missing teeth affect more than your smile — they change how you eat, speak, and feel
about yourself. The good news: at Hilltop Family Dentistry in Casper, you have excellent
options. The two most common are dental implants and dentures, and the right choice
depends on your goals, your jaw health, and your budget.

## Dental implants

A dental implant is a small titanium post that acts as a new tooth root, topped with a
custom crown. It's the most natural-feeling, longest-lasting option.

- Looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth
- Can last a lifetime with good care
- Doesn't rely on or damage neighboring teeth
- Helps preserve the jawbone

Implants are ideal for replacing one tooth, several teeth, or even securing a full
denture.

## Dentures

Modern dentures are a comfortable, affordable way to replace several or all of your
teeth.

- Full dentures replace a complete arch
- Partial dentures fill gaps while preserving your natural teeth
- Implant-supported dentures snap onto implants for a secure fit that won't slip
