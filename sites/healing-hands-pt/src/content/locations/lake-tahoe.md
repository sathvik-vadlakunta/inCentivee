The Lake Tahoe basin — from Incline and Kings Beach on the north shore down toward the South Lake area — is full of active people who push their bodies hard year-round. When pain gets in the way, Lake Tahoe clients come to Healing Hands Physical Therapy & Bodywork in South Reno for **a full hour of one-on-one, hands-on care** with a doctor of physical therapy who treats the **root cause**, not just the symptom.

## Getting here from the lake

Our clinic at **9460 Double R Blvd, #104** in South Reno is roughly **45–60 minutes** from most of the Lake Tahoe basin, depending on your starting point and the season. In winter, allow extra time for mountain conditions and drive safely.

Honest note: our **mobile (in-home) service covers Reno-Sparks only**, so Lake Tahoe clients are seen **in our clinic**. We won't promise mobile visits we can't reliably deliver over the passes.

## Care built for Tahoe athletes and weekend warriors

Skiing, paddleboarding, mountain biking, trail running — Tahoe life is demanding. Concierge cash-pay care means **your plan is built by you and Dr. Jamie together**, with a full hour of focused hands-on work each visit, so you recover faster and get back on the water and the trails.

## Services available

Dr. Jamie Pribyl, PT, DPT, MTC, brings 15+ years of manual-therapy experience treating back and neck pain, headaches, TMJ, shoulder, hip, and knee pain, tendonitis, and post-operative recovery — using manual therapy and bodywork, dry needling, CranioSacral therapy, StemWave, myofascial release, muscle energy technique, cupping, and visceral mobilization.
