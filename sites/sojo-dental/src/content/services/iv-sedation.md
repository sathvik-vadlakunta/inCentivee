Dental anxiety should never prevent you from getting the care you need. SoJo Dental offers IV sedation for patients who experience fear or anxiety about dental procedures. [Research shows that approximately 42% of adults experience moderate dental anxiety, with 12% experiencing extreme fear](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41415-024-7846-1) — which is why safe, effective sedation options are so important for comprehensive dental care.

## Who Benefits from IV Sedation?

- Patients with dental anxiety or phobia
- Those undergoing lengthy or complex procedures
- Patients with a strong gag reflex
- Individuals with difficulty getting numb with local anesthesia alone

## How It Works

A small IV line delivers sedation medication directly into your bloodstream. You'll feel deeply relaxed and comfortable throughout your procedure. Most patients have little or no memory of the treatment afterward.

Dr. Brian Call is trained and certified in IV sedation administration, ensuring your safety and comfort throughout every procedure.
