Ryse provides in-home and community-based ABA for individuals **ages 2 through 65** with an autism diagnosis. Most providers stop at young children — we don't. Whether you're supporting a toddler just beginning their journey, a school-age child, a teen, or an adult, our care adapts to the person in front of us. Every plan is designed and overseen by a BCBA and built around goals that matter to your family — never a one-size-fits-all template. ## Insurance and no waitlist

Two things stop most families from starting ABA: long waitlists and insurance confusion. Ryse removes both. **We have no waitlist — St. George families can start right away.** And we handle the insurance legwork for you. ABA is widely recognized as an evidence-based autism service, and many Utah plans cover it. Utah's autism insurance mandate, expanded effective January 2020, requires state-regulated individual and large-group plans to cover autism treatment for individuals of any age, with no age or dollar caps on ABA ([Autism Speaks](https://www.autismspeaks.org/advocacy-news/utah-autism-insurance-mandate-expanded)). Utah Medicaid also covers ABA for eligible children with a valid autism diagnosis ([Utah Medicaid](https://medicaid-manuals.dhhs.utah.gov/Autism_Spectrum_Services/autism_spectrum_services.htm)). Coverage details vary by plan, so we run a **free benefits check** and confirm exactly what your insurance covers before we begin. Eligibility note, shared honestly: an autism diagnosis and active coverage are required to start.
