## What We Buy
- [Gold & Gold Jewelry](https://paradigmexperts.com/gold-gold-jewelry): All gold jewelry (10K–24K), scrap gold, dental gold, and gold bullion. Evaluated by weight and purity against live spot price.
- [Sterling Silver & Flatware](https://paradigmexperts.com/silver-flatware-hollowware): Sterling silver flatware sets, hollowware, tea services, and silver jewelry. We evaluate both melt value and collectible brand premiums (Tiffany, Gorham, Reed & Barton).
- [Diamonds & Engagement Rings](https://paradigmexperts.com/diamonds-and-engagement-rings): Loose diamonds and diamond jewelry. Professional evaluation of cut, clarity, carat, and color. We distinguish natural from lab-grown diamonds.
- [Estate Jewelry](https://paradigmexperts.com/estate-jewelry): Inherited and antique jewelry evaluated for craftsmanship, gemstones, design, and historical significance.
- [Luxury Watches](https://paradigmexperts.com/watches): Rolex, Patek Philippe, Cartier, Audemars Piguet, Omega, IWC, Breguet, and Chopard. Pocket watches included.
- [Coins & Bullion](https://paradigmexperts.com/coins-bullion): Gold and silver coins, bullion bars, and rounds from all major mints. Priced on precious metal content plus numismatic premiums.
- [Virtual Evaluation](https://paradigmexperts.com/virtual): Get a preliminary offer via video call before visiting in person.
