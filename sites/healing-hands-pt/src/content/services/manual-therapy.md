## Hands-on care that finds the root cause

At Healing Hands, manual therapy and bodywork are at the center of everything we do. We spend most of our time analyzing your **gait, posture, and mobility** to determine the *root cause* of the problem — then we use hands-on techniques to get you moving your best, decrease compensatory movements, and improve your function.

## Techniques we use

- **Joint mobilizations** — restore mobility and range of motion to restricted joints
- **Soft-tissue mobilization** — release tight and restricted tissue
- **Myofascial release** — free up fascial restrictions that limit movement
- **Muscle energy technique** — gentle, you-powered joint mobilization
- **Strain/counterstrain** — position-based release of tender points
- **CranioSacral therapy** — light-touch work for the central nervous system

## Why bodywork first

Our holistic approach won't have you doing repetitive exercises in the gym several times a week. We focus on **bodywork first** so you get relief from pain — then layer in targeted home exercises so the results last.
