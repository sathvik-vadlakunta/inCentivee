Good / Better / Best Pricing Model + Competitive Analysis

Rebuilds our pricing around real vendor costs (FATJOE link building, citations, brand listicles) and 2026 market benchmarks. Drops the $500/mo Starter (too low, too thin to deliver real value), and sets a defensible three-tier ladder that centers our ~$3k/mo target ACV while keeping 65–88% gross margin.

Owner: Kody · companion to link-building-pricing.html + fatjoe-va-ops-and-tracking.html · updates email 08-pricing-quote.md · 2026-06-19

TL;DR

  • Kill the $500 Starter. It's below the credible market floor for local SEO, can't fund real link building, and trains clients to expect cheap. Market data shows the working median for single-location local SEO is $1,000–$2,000/mo, and service verticals like dental already pay $2,000–$2,500.
  • New ladder: $1,500 → $2,500 → $3,500/mo (good / better / best), each tied to a website treatment (optimize / migrate / redesign) AND a clear escalation of content + links + GEO.
  • GEO/AEO is the price justifier. It's a category competitors sell separately for $1k–$3k/mo (WebFX bundles GEO at $3,000). Folding it into every tier lets us price at the top of the local-SEO band.
  • Margin lives in links + GEO. Citations & GBP are bundled table-stakes (cheap). Link building carries ~2–3× markup over FATJOE wholesale; GEO is high-perceived-value and largely automated by our geo_agent.

5 · Why drop the $500 tier

  • Below the credible floor. $500 is beneath the agency working median; it signals "cheap SEO," which is exactly the bargain-content segment we don't want to compete in.
  • Can't fund real value. A single DR30 link ($120) + citations + GEO already eats a big chunk; there's no room to deliver the link building that actually moves authority. It forces thin deliverables that underperform the 90-day guarantee.
  • Anchoring. A $500 floor drags the whole ladder down and makes $1,500 feel expensive. Removing it makes $1,500 the entry and $2,500 (Grow) the obvious popular choice — pulling ACV toward our $3k target.
  • Migration path: existing $500 clients → move to Optimize $1,500 at renewal (grandfather for one cycle if needed), framed as "we've added real link building + GEO and dropped the entry tier."
