Root canal treatment often gets an unfair reputation, but modern endodontic therapy is highly successful and far more comfortable than many patients expect. [Root canal treatment has a success rate of approximately 95%, preserving the natural tooth for a lifetime of function](https://www.aae.org/patients/root-canal-treatment/), according to the American Association of Endodontists.

## Understanding Root Canal Success Rates

The 95% success rate for root canal treatment represents one of the highest success rates in dentistry. This statistic reflects both immediate treatment success and long-term tooth preservation when patients follow proper aftercare and maintenance.

At Sojo Dental, we've observed that patient understanding of the procedure significantly impacts both comfort levels and treatment outcomes. When South Jordan patients know what to expect, they approach treatment with less anxiety and better compliance with post-treatment care instructions.

### Factors Contributing to High Success Rates:

- **Advanced diagnostic technology:** Digital imaging allows precise treatment planning
- **Modern anesthesia techniques:** Ensure complete comfort throughout the procedure
- **Improved instrumentation:** Rotary files and electronic apex locators increase precision
- **Enhanced disinfection protocols:** Better eliminate bacteria from root canal systems
- **Superior sealing materials:** Prevent recontamination of treated roots

## Root Canal Treatment FAQ

### How painful is root canal treatment?

Modern root canal treatment is typically no more uncomfortable than having a large filling placed. With effective local anesthesia, patients should feel no pain during the procedure. Most patients experience only mild discomfort for a few days afterward, easily managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

### What is the success rate of root canal retreatment?

Root canal retreatment has a success rate of approximately 85-90% when performed by experienced endodontists. While slightly lower than initial treatment, retreatment often provides an excellent opportunity to save a tooth that might otherwise require extraction and replacement.

### How long do root canal treated teeth last?

With proper restoration and care, root canal treated teeth can last a lifetime. The key factors are prompt placement of a permanent restoration (usually a crown) and maintaining good oral hygiene. Most root canal treated teeth function normally for decades when properly cared for.

### What are signs that root canal treatment has failed?

Signs of root canal failure include persistent pain, swelling, sensitivity to temperature, or the development of a pimple-like bump on the gum. However, some failed root canals may be asymptomatic, which is why regular dental checkups with X-rays are important for monitoring treated teeth.

## Modern Root Canal Techniques in 2026

Root canal treatment has evolved significantly in recent years, with new technologies improving both success rates and patient comfort. At Sojo Dental, we utilize state-of-the-art equipment and techniques to ensure optimal outcomes for our South Jordan patients.

### Advanced Technologies We Use:

- **Digital radiography:** Provides detailed images with minimal radiation exposure
- **Electronic apex locators:** Precisely determine root canal length without additional X-rays
- **Rotary instrumentation:** Cleans and shapes root canals more efficiently and thoroughly
- **Ultrasonic activation:** Enhances disinfection of the root canal system
- **Biocompatible filling materials:** Provide superior sealing and tissue compatibility

## Root Canal vs. Tooth Extraction: Making the Right Choice

Given the high success rate of root canal treatment, preservation of the natural tooth is almost always preferable to extraction when possible. Natural teeth provide better chewing efficiency and maintain proper oral function compared to replacement options.

However, not every tooth is a candidate for root canal treatment. Our team at Sojo Dental carefully evaluates each case to determine whether root canal therapy offers the best long-term prognosis for our South Jordan patients.

### Factors Favoring Root Canal Treatment:

- Adequate remaining tooth structure
- Healthy periodontal support
- Strategic importance in the dental arch
- Patient preference to retain natural tooth
- Cost considerations compared to replacement options
