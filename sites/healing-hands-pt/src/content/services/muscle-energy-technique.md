## Gentle, you-powered joint mobilization

Muscle energy technique (MET) is a **gentle and subtle** manual-therapy approach. It requires you to use just about **2 lbs of force**, contracting your own muscles to improve joint mobility — which makes it safe, comfortable, and surprisingly effective.

## What MET can do

- **Reduce pain**
- **Balance muscle tone**
- **Stretch tight muscles** and **strengthen weak muscles**
- **Improve circulation**
- **Mobilize joint restrictions**

MET is one of several hands-on tools we combine to get to the root cause of your pain.
