Gum disease is one of the most common — and most overlooked — health problems in the
country. It's also the **leading cause of tooth loss in adults**. The good news: when it's
caught early, it's very treatable. Here's what every Casper family should know.

## Just how common is it?

More common than most people realize. According to the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/about/gum-periodontal-disease.html),
**42% of adults aged 30 and older have some form of periodontal (gum) disease**, and that
number climbs to **over 70% of adults aged 65 and older**. Many of them have no idea,
because early gum disease rarely hurts.

## Warning signs to watch for

See us if you notice any of these:

- **Gums that bleed** when you brush or floss
- **Red, swollen, or tender** gums
- **Persistent bad breath** or a bad taste
- **Receding gums** (teeth looking "longer")
- **Loose teeth** or a change in your bite

## Why it matters beyond your mouth

Gum disease doesn't stay in your mouth. Research has linked periodontal disease to broader
health concerns including heart disease and diabetes complications. Protecting your gums is
part of protecting your overall health.

## How we treat it at Hilltop

Treatment depends on the stage:

- **Early (gingivitis):** improved cleanings and home care often reverse it
- **Established (periodontitis):** a deep cleaning called **scaling and root planing**
  removes bacteria below the gumline
- **Ongoing:** periodontal maintenance visits keep it from coming back

Our team makes every step comfortable, and we'll coach you on the home routine that keeps
your gums healthy between visits.
