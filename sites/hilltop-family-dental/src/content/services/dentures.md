Dentures remain one of the most affordable, reliable ways to replace missing teeth. At Hilltop, we craft natural-looking dentures designed for a comfortable, confident fit.

## You're not alone

Tooth loss is far more common than most people realize. According to the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/about/about-tooth-loss.html), **about half of U.S. adults aged 20–64 have lost at least one permanent tooth**, and roughly **15% of adults 65 and older have lost all of their natural teeth**. Modern dentures — especially implant-supported options — make it easier than ever to eat, speak, and smile with confidence again.

## Your denture options

- **Full dentures** — replace a complete arch of missing teeth
- **Partial dentures** — fill gaps while preserving your remaining natural teeth
- **Implant-supported dentures** — snap securely onto dental implants for a fit that won't slip

## Comfortable and natural-looking

We fit your dentures precisely and adjust them as you settle in, so eating and speaking feel natural. If you want added stability, ask us about implant-supported options that also help preserve the jawbone.

**Sources:** [CDC — Tooth Loss](https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/about/about-tooth-loss.html).
