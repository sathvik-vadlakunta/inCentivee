When it comes to children's oral health, precision and safety matter. At SoJo Dental, our advanced pediatric digital X-rays help us diagnose and treat dental concerns with exceptional accuracy and care.

## What Are Pediatric Digital X-Rays?

Pediatric digital X-rays are high-resolution images that help our team detect issues such as cavities, impacted teeth, misalignment or early signs of disease. These images are captured using a small digital sensor placed gently in the mouth. Unlike traditional X-rays, the results are available instantly and use up to 90 percent less radiation.

## The Digital X-Ray Experience

1. Our dentists explain the procedure to your child to help reduce any anxiety
2. The sensor is gently placed in the mouth to capture detailed images
3. Images are instantly available on-screen, allowing for immediate review
4. Our team identifies potential concerns such as decay or structural issues
5. We discuss findings and treatment options with you in a clear, supportive manner

## Why Digital X-Rays Are a Smart Choice

- **Detects hidden cavities** and structural issues early
- **Reduces radiation exposure** significantly compared to traditional methods
- **Monitors growth patterns** and tooth development
- **Supports precise treatment planning** for timely care

By catching potential concerns early, we can help prevent more complex dental problems in the future.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are pediatric digital X-rays safe for young children?**

Yes, we use low-dose radiation and protective measures like lead aprons to ensure safety.

**Do children need digital X-rays at every visit?**

Not always. Our dentists will recommend them only when necessary to support diagnosis or monitoring.

**Can digital X-rays show new tooth development?**

Absolutely. They help us track emerging teeth and assess space and alignment.

**Is there any preparation required for digital X-rays?**

No special preparation is needed. The procedure is quick and child-friendly.
