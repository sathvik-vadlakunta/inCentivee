## Gold & Gold Jewelry
**URL**: https://paradigmexperts.com/gold-gold-jewelry

Paradigm Experts buys all forms of gold including jewelry (10K through 24K), scrap gold, dental gold, and gold bullion. Every piece is evaluated by weight and purity, then priced against the live spot price from Kitco.com. We pay up to 90% of melt value — among the highest payouts in the Northern Virginia area.

Common items we buy:
- Gold rings, necklaces, bracelets, and earrings (any karat)
- Broken or damaged gold jewelry
- Dental gold (crowns, bridges, and fillings)
- Gold bullion bars and rounds
- Scrap gold from any source

Our evaluation process is transparent: we test purity with professional-grade XRF analyzers, weigh items on certified scales, and show you the live market price so you understand exactly how your offer is calculated.

## Sterling Silver & Flatware
**URL**: https://paradigmexperts.com/silver-flatware-hollowware

We purchase sterling silver flatware, hollowware, tea services, serving pieces, and silver jewelry. Items are evaluated for both precious metal melt value and collectible brand premiums. Premium brands like Tiffany & Co., Gorham, Reed & Barton, Wallace, and International Silver often command prices well above melt value.

Common items we buy:
- Complete and partial flatware sets
- Tea sets and coffee services
- Serving trays, bowls, and candlesticks
- Silver jewelry and accessories
- Damaged or tarnished sterling silver (tarnish does not reduce value)

We test all items to confirm sterling silver content (.925 purity) and evaluate brand, pattern, condition, and completeness when determining value.

## Diamonds & Engagement Rings
**URL**: https://paradigmexperts.com/diamonds-and-engagement-rings

Our diamond evaluation covers the 4Cs — cut, clarity, carat weight, and color — using professional gemological equipment. We buy loose diamonds, diamond engagement rings, tennis bracelets, and diamond-set jewelry of all types. We distinguish natural diamonds from lab-grown diamonds and evaluate each accordingly.

What sets us apart:
- Professional gemological evaluation equipment
- We explain our assessment of each stone
- Natural and lab-grown diamonds evaluated separately
- No minimum carat weight
- Settings evaluated separately for precious metal content

## Estate Jewelry
**URL**: https://paradigmexperts.com/estate-jewelry

Inherited, antique, and vintage jewelry requires specialized knowledge. We evaluate estate pieces for craftsmanship, gemstone quality, design significance, maker's marks, and historical context — not just metal weight. This means pieces with artistic or historical value receive offers that reflect their true worth, not just their scrap value.

We regularly work with:
- Families settling estates
- Estate sale companies and professional organizers
- Individuals who have inherited jewelry collections
- Downsizing clients

All evaluations are private, no-pressure, and obligation-free.
