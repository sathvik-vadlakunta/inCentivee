When a cavity is detected, a dental filling restores the tooth to its normal shape and function. At SoJo Dental, we use tooth-colored composite resin fillings that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

## Why Tooth-Colored Fillings?

- **Natural appearance** — matches your tooth color
- **Mercury-free** — safe composite resin material
- **Bonds to tooth structure** — strengthens the remaining tooth
- **Less drilling** — preserves more natural tooth structure than amalgam
- **Versatile** — repairs chips, cracks, and worn teeth in addition to cavities

## The Filling Process

1. The decayed portion of the tooth is removed
2. The area is cleaned and prepared
3. Composite resin is applied in layers and shaped
4. A curing light hardens the material
5. The filling is polished for a smooth, natural finish

Most fillings are completed in a single, comfortable appointment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can dental fillings fall out over time?**

Yes, fillings can wear down or loosen. Regular checkups help detect and correct issues early.

**Are composite fillings as strong as amalgam?**

Composite fillings are strong, especially for front or visible teeth, though amalgam may last longer in high-stress areas.

**Do dental fillings affect sensitivity?**

Some patients experience mild sensitivity after placement. This usually resolves within a few days.

**Is there a limit to how many fillings I can get?**

There is no strict limit, but multiple fillings may require staged appointments to ensure comfort and proper healing.

**Which areas do you serve for dental fillings?**

We provide dental fillings in South Jordan, Utah, and for the surrounding areas of Herriman, Riverton and West Jordan, Utah.
