## Gentle care for the nervous system

CranioSacral therapy (CST) is a **light-touch therapy** that assists the natural movement of cerebrospinal fluid, allowing for optimal neurological healing. By gently easing restrictions around the central nervous system, CST helps:

- Relieve tension in the **central nervous system (CNS)**
- Promote a deep sense of well-being and calm
- Reduce pain
- Support overall health and immunity
