At SoJo Dental, we offer pediatric tooth-colored dental crowns as a natural-looking, comfortable solution for protecting and restoring young teeth affected by decay or damage. Unlike traditional metal options, tooth-colored crowns are made from durable, biocompatible materials that blend with a child's natural tooth color.

## What Are Pediatric Tooth-Colored Crowns?

Tooth-colored pediatric crowns are used when a child's tooth has been damaged due to extensive decay or trauma. The procedure begins with careful removal of decay, followed by shaping the tooth and placing a custom-fitted crown. These crowns are ideal for both primary and permanent teeth, depending on the situation.

## Why Parents Choose Tooth-Colored Crowns

- **Protects weak or decayed teeth** and prevents further damage
- **Natural appearance** — mimics natural tooth color and contour for a seamless look
- **Minimally invasive** — procedure with minimal discomfort
- **Long-lasting** — designed to withstand daily wear

Children often feel more confident when their restored tooth looks just like the rest, and parents appreciate the durability and functionality these crowns provide.

## Our Approach to Pediatric Care

We prioritize your child's comfort and peace of mind with kid-friendly techniques, calm environments and the latest in pediatric dental technology. With good care, pediatric crowns typically last until the tooth is naturally lost or replaced with a permanent one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are tooth-colored crowns safe for children?**

Yes, we use materials that are strong, biocompatible and approved for pediatric use.

**Can baby teeth get crowns?**

Absolutely. Crowns are often used on baby teeth to protect them until they naturally fall out.

**Do children need anesthesia for crown placement?**

We use local anesthesia and offer comfort options to ensure your child feels at ease throughout the procedure.

**How long do pediatric crowns last?**

With good care, they typically last until the tooth is naturally lost or replaced with a permanent one.
