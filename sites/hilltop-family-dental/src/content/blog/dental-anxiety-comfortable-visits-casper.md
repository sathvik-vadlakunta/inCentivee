If the thought of a dental appointment makes you tense up, you're not alone — and you're
always welcome at Hilltop Family Dentistry. Dental anxiety is one of the most common reasons
people put off care, and we've built our practice around making visits feel different.

## You're in good company

Dental fear is genuinely widespread. A systematic review and meta-analysis estimates that
about **15% of adults experience dental anxiety**, with roughly **12% reporting high dental
fear** — and among people seeking emergency dental care, that figure climbs to nearly
**half**. It's one of the most common reasons people delay treatment. Our patients tell us
about it all the time, and our team is experienced, patient, and completely judgment-free
about it.

## How we keep you comfortable

We believe gentle, unhurried care is the best anxiety medicine. At every visit we:

- **Listen first** — tell us what worries you, and we'll go at your pace
- **Explain everything** — no surprises; you always know what's happening and why
- **Go gently** — modern, minimally invasive techniques mean less discomfort
- **Offer breaks** — there's no rushing; you're in control

## Sedation options when you need them

For patients who want extra help relaxing, we offer **nitrous oxide** ("laughing gas") — a
safe, gentle option that helps you feel calm during treatment and wears off within minutes,
so you can drive yourself home afterward.

## Don't let fear cost you your health

Avoiding the dentist tends to turn small, easy-to-treat issues into bigger ones. The kindest
thing you can do for your smile — and your peace of mind — is to be seen regularly by a team
that *gets it*. Many of our most anxious patients tell us they finally found a dental home
here.
