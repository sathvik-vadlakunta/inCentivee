Dr. Isabel Scott is an Iowa native who earned her dental degree from the University of Iowa College of Dentistry before completing a General Practice Residency at the Salt Lake City Veterans Affairs Medical Center. There, she received advanced training in implants, oral surgery, comprehensive restorative care, and cosmetic dentistry.

When she's not caring for patients, Dr. Scott enjoys golfing with her husband, training for marathons, and exploring everything Utah has to offer. She is excited to serve the community and help patients at SoJo Dental achieve healthy, confident smiles.
