"I think patients deserve comfortable care in the most efficient way." That philosophy guides everything Dr. David Gallup does at Hilltop Family Dentistry.

Dr. Gallup settled in Casper after graduating from Indiana University School of Dentistry, drawn to the chance to build lasting relationships with the families he cares for. He combines a gentle, patient-first approach with modern techniques to make every visit comfortable and efficient — whether it's a routine checkup, a cosmetic makeover, or restoring a smile with dental implants.

Patients appreciate that Dr. Gallup takes the time to listen, explain their options clearly, and deliver honest, high-quality care.
