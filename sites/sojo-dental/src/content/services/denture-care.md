Properly maintained dentures provide years of comfortable, reliable function. At SoJo Dental, we offer ongoing denture care services to ensure your prosthetics fit well and look natural throughout their lifespan.

## Our Denture Care Services

- **Adjustments** — fine-tuning the fit to eliminate sore spots and improve comfort
- **Relines** — reshaping the interior of the denture to match changes in your gums and bone
- **Repairs** — fixing cracks, chips, or broken clasps
- **Replacements** — fabricating new dentures when existing ones have worn out

## At-Home Denture Care Tips

- Remove and rinse dentures after eating
- Brush dentures daily with a soft-bristled brush and denture cleaner (not regular toothpaste)
- Soak dentures overnight in a denture cleaning solution
- Handle dentures carefully — place a towel in the sink when cleaning
- Continue regular dental check-ups even with full dentures
- Clean your gums, tongue, and palate daily before inserting dentures

Regular professional check-ups help us monitor the fit of your dentures and the health of your gums and remaining teeth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are partial dentures available for younger patients?**

Yes, partial dentures are a great option for young adults missing only a few teeth, offering a natural look and a comfortable feel.

**How do I know if my dentures need an adjustment?**

If you experience pain, slipping, clicking or difficulty eating, it is time to have your dentures assessed for a possible adjustment.

**Can digital tools really improve denture fit?**

Absolutely. Digital scanning allows for more precise measurements, resulting in dentures that fit better and feel more natural.

**Is emergency denture repair offered?**

We do offer urgent repairs when available. Please call our office immediately to check availability for emergency care.

**Which areas do you serve outside South Jordan, Utah?**
